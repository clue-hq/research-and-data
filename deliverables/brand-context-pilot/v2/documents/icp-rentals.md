## Qualification decisions

1. **Operator class.** STR/vacation-rental manager versus hotel-led operation. For serviced apartments and mixed portfolios, establish staffed front-desk versus distributed listing operations before selecting the hotel or rental motion; do not transfer STR case metrics automatically. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing)
2. **Who runs communications.** If listing owners never touch guest threads, the manager is the account.
3. **Channels and reservation context.** Inbox lists SMS, WhatsApp, email, calls, social messages, and connected OTA threads, with PMS/OTA details beside the conversation. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/inbox)
4. **Scale.** No published minimum units, revenue, or headcount. 100 / 108 / 170+ are **examples**, not cutoffs. Startup exists for 0–25 units. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing)
5. **Proof.** One named story, dates and caveats in the same breath. Do not average 60% / 70% / 80%. See [Customer evidence & usable proof](#knowledge/customer-evidence).

Research without projecting pain: management brand vs owner name on OTAs; PMS mentioned in careers or help center; advertised 24/7 guest line; Intercom/Zendesk vs Hostaway/Guesty messaging; multi-market sites. Then ask—playbook: [Qualification & account research playbook](#knowledge/buying-triggers).

## Signals and next questions

**Public signals to investigate (not auto-qualify):** multiple listings under one management brand; Hostaway/Guesty/Lodgify in public stack clues; WhatsApp as the guest line; overnight phone coverage; a recent PM acquisition; owner-communication copy.

**Next questions:** Which system owns Airbnb/Vrbo threads today? Separate owner vs guest vs vendor queues? Who approves AI vs autopilot? Are smart locks, waivers, or deposits on the critical path? Is housekeeping coordination a now-need while Operations is still “coming soon” on the nav?
