## What this document means

This brief is about the work a hospitality buyer still has to get done and the result they would count as success. It is not a second catalog of [AI Agents](#knowledge/product-agents), [Inbox](#knowledge/product-inbox), [Guest Portals](#knowledge/product-portals), or [Workflows](#knowledge/product-workflows). Those surfaces can sit inside a job; they are not the job.

A desired KPI (share of conversations closed without a person, minutes to reply, support FTE, ancillary revenue, NOI) is what an operator would watch. It is not a promised result for the next account. Named figures below are Conduit-attributed stories collected in [Customer evidence & usable proof](#knowledge/customer-evidence). Use one named customer per motion; do not average rates. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/haven-2026)

Public FAQ copy says Conduit is for hotel teams, serviced-apartment operators, and short-term rental managers, with agents that answer messages and calls, handle routine requests, and coordinate with the team. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/industry/independent-hotels)

## Work buyers need to accomplish

The recurring job is: keep every guest conversation answered with the right property and booking context, complete the stay (access, payments, extras), and grow units or nights without a matching night desk.

| Job | Situation | Workaround | Failure | Capability in the job | Change they would measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cover threads without a night desk | After-hours calls, lockouts, late check-in | Call center, Google Voice, VA shifts | Hour waits; owner still in Slack | Agents on connected voice/chat/email/WhatsApp/OTA paths with available reservation context; confirm channel routing and supported actions per connector; escalate with history | First-response time; overnight hours covered; human issues per incident |
| One workspace for OTA + messaging + calls | Multi-channel STR or hotel | Two inboxes (e.g. Intercom + Hostaway) plus WhatsApp | Lost context; no performance view | Inbox with booking details beside the thread | Channels unified; time spent switching tools |
| Add units without linear guest-support hiring | 40→100+ listings or an acquisition | Hire per shift as volume climbs | Backlogs (e.g. 630 unread); model does not scale | Agents + workflows + knowledge teaching | Automation %; properties per support rep; hires avoided |
| Get guests arrival-ready without another app | Codes, deposits, waivers, extras | Email sequences, PDF guides, extra payment links | Guests still ask; access delayed | Portal: one branded stay link | Pre-arrival completion; inbound “how do I get in” volume |
| Offer extras in the live conversation | Unused rooms, gap nights, early check-in, spa | Pre-arrival upgrade emails | Independent-hotel page says those emails convert below 10% | Workflows/agents using booking rules | Ancillary attach—not a guaranteed lift |

## Before and after workflows

**Scenario 1 — STR growth is a staffing problem.** Haven Vacation Rentals managed roughly 270 Smoky Mountain listings. Peak guest support was about 25 people across shifts. Conduit was already installed near 40% automation with few workflows; volume still scaled with units. After the team standardized answers and taught the system, the 2026 write-up reports 90% automation, two people per shift, 14 January listings without extra support hires, and scores held around 4.8–4.9. In a snowstorm with 90 same-day check-ins, three issues needed a human. That is Haven’s attributed path, not a template rate. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/haven-2026)

HomeHop’s same job after a failed AI vendor and a Google Voice lockout: roughly 40 to 108 listings without extra guest-support hires, 70% automation, and voice coverage 1 AM–7 AM. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/homehop)

**Scenario 2 — Boutique hotel as a hands-off asset.** The Lauderdale Boutique Hotel had cycled through Jurny’s PMS/call center and other hospitality call services: costs from $4,000 down to $1,200 a month, guests waiting up to an hour, the owner still answering Slack, refunds mounting. Desired result was investor-grade, low-touch operations. The story reports support at $600/month, average response 57 minutes to 2 minutes, NOI up at least $3,400/month from costs and fewer refunds, and an 8% cap-rate translation of that NOI into more than $500,000 of modeled asset value. The write-up describes an AI platform plus a 24/7 staffed support team, so the software-only contribution is not isolated. Cap-rate math is the owner’s model, not an appraisal. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/lauderdale-hotel)

**Separate STR acquisition example — Cascadia Getaways.** The vacation-rental manager reports 60% automation and a 4.9 communication score across 100 properties. During acquisition integration, leadership expressed confidence about adding properties without extra guest-service hires; this is an expectation, not a completed staffing result or hotel proof. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/cascadia-getaways)

## Turning problems into campaigns

Lead with the job, then one named proof, then the surface. Drafts must not invent a relationship or claim every customer shares the pain. Writing rules: [Campaign writing examples & rules](#knowledge/writing-examples), [Audience-to-message map](#knowledge/message-map).

- **Cannot hire as fast as units.** Haven or HomeHop; ask current automation %, overnight headcount, and whether policies live in one knowledge base. [ICP · vacation-rental operators](#knowledge/icp-rentals).
- **Guests wait; owner still in Slack.** Lauderdale minutes and support cost; confirm call-center spend and refund leakage. [ICP · hotel operators](#knowledge/icp-hotels).
- **Two inboxes.** Bocobay left Intercom to unify WhatsApp, email, phone, and live chat. The page header “80% resolution” / “100s hours” is not derived in the body—do not lead with it. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/bocobay)
- **After hours is the real desk.** Independent-hotel after-hours language plus HomeHop 1–7 AM voice. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/industry/independent-hotels)
- **Money left on extras.** Workflows can offer early check-in, gap nights, and upgrades; Cosmica’s ~5% Dubai figure is one market’s peak. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/workflows.md)

This pack has no hotel-group P&L body, no isolated Portals outcome, and no shared automation denominator. Investigate those before writing group-wide guarantees. Capability mapping: [Product catalog & capability map](#knowledge/products-capabilities).
