## Fit, readiness and timing

**Fit.** The pricing FAQ states Conduit is for hotel teams, serviced-apartment operators, and short-term rental managers; agents answer guest messages and calls, handle routine requests, and coordinate work with the team. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing) Site industries are short-term rentals, independent hotels, and hotel groups. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents) Positive observation: the account sells or manages stays and already talks to guests on channels the inbox unifies. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/inbox.md) Hotels and STR operators are both in-category and different tests (after-hours desk, Opera/Mews/Cloudbeds, spa/dining extras versus per-listing knowledge, Airbnb threads, check-in and booking-change requests). [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/industry/independent-hotels) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/industry/short-term-rentals) Unknown: keep serviced apartments and hotel groups in-category until disproven—named stories in this pack cluster in STR/PM companies, with one boutique-hotel write-up. Disqualify only when the company does not operate those stays. A motivated champion does not create fit; titles belong in [Buying committee & professional personas](#knowledge/buyer-roles).

**Readiness.** Agents retrieve context and take action in connected tools, then escalate. How much they resolve “depends on your guest requests, knowledge base, integrations, and escalation rules.” [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents.md) Haven already had Conduit installed at about 40 percent automation with listings uploaded, no workflows, and minimal knowledge-base work—so a live install is not readiness. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/haven-2026) Confirm whether a PMS path actually supplies live reservation context and whether messaging rides that connector. Opera Cloud is described as reservation and profile sync; it does not route guest messaging through that integration. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/integrations/opera) Booking.com and VRBO were marked coming soon on the captured directory. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/integrations) A logo is not a supported action.

**Timing versus later outcomes.** Named situations to investigate, not needs every customer has: guest volume versus support seats (Haven, HomeHop, Canbnb); overnight voice (HomeHop 1–7 AM; independent-hotel after-hours copy); split inboxes or generic helpdesk misfit (Bocobay leaving Intercom); acquisition or multi-market expansion (Cascadia, Cosmica, Wynwood); owner support-cost / NOI (Lauderdale). [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/haven-2026) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/lauderdale-hotel) “We cannot cover the next peak, night shift, or acquired portfolio” is buying timing. “We want 90% automation” or cap-rate math is a later adoption or outcome claim—do not use it as a qualification bar.

## Discovery conversation

Collect observations that fill the matrix. Do not quote attributed rates as typical.

Draft questions:

1. Who operates the stays, and is the contract with the owner, manager, or brand?
2. Which channels must sit in one workspace on day one?
3. Which system is source of truth for reservations, and should guest messaging stay on the PMS or move?
4. Who owns property policies, and who reviews agent handoffs?
5. What breaks if you add listings, an overnight window, or an acquired portfolio without new support seats? Haven reported adding 14 listings in January without adding support staff—ask whether that pressure exists; do not promise the result. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/haven-2026)
6. Is the job unified inbox and voice (Messaging starting $18/unit/mo), portals/waivers/payments (Guest starting $30), devices, or Full Suite operational features? Operator is the workspace assistant; Operations is a separate coming-soon product label. Confirm the exact operational feature before quoting a delivery commitment. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing)

Separate current pain (unread backlog, hour-long replies, two inboxes, Slack as the night desk) from later desired state (automation share, NOI, 24/7 autopilot).
