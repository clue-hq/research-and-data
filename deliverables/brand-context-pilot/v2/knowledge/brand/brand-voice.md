# Brand voice & language

This is an inferred voice from public Conduit pages, product copy, the manifesto, customer stories, and LinkedIn ads—not a signed style guide. Pair it with [Positioning & brand promise](#knowledge/brand-messaging), [Company overview](#knowledge/company-overview), and [Customer evidence & usable proof](#knowledge/customer-evidence). Proposed lines below are **drafts**. Do not present them as published Conduit copy or as a relationship with a named prospect.

## Voice principles

**Write for the operator, not for “AI.”** Homepage and product pages open on guest messages, calls, bookings, handoffs, and procedures, then name the product. Industries on the same pages are short-term rentals, independent hotels, and hotel groups. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/)

**Agents act, then people take the edges.** Product language is a relay, not a replacement slogan: agents answer and take action in connected tools; they escalate when needed; the inbox lets the team take over with history. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/inbox)

**The agent mirrors the operation.** The manifesto is the longest official voice: coordination layer, full context across channels, specialists stay, the middle shuttle role goes away—and a sloppy operation yields a sloppy agent. That is founder essay, denser than product UI copy; do not flatten it into a homepage guarantee. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/manifesto)

**Hospitality-specific, not generic desk software.** LinkedIn paid units contrast “AI agents built for hospitality” with generic chatbots and “human touch.” Use that contrast only as category language, not as a claim that every reply is indistinguishable from a host. [Source](https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/detail/1538690886) [Source](https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/detail/1538623406)

**Precision over borrowed superlatives.** Product blocks use numbered jobs and parallel verbs (answer, act, review). Scale lines conflict in public: homepage “300+ hospitality brands in 120+ countries” versus a LinkedIn ad’s “1,000+ … in 120+ countries.” Attribute company reach; do not pick a number to sound bigger. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/) [Source](https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/detail/1538690886)

**Proof stays named.** Customer pages and ads retell Haven, Wynwood House, Cosmica, Lauderdale, and others with operator quotes and local numbers. Voice may be warmer there than on product FAQs; still keep the customer, metric, and caveat in one breath. See [Customer evidence & usable proof](#knowledge/customer-evidence).

## Vocabulary and sentence patterns

Observed pattern (public copy) → writing rule → **draft** example → avoid.

| Pattern in sources | Rule | Draft (not published copy) | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Two-beat imperatives: “Answer the question. Handle the request.” [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents) | Lead with the guest job, then the system action. | Draft: Answer the WhatsApp. Update the reservation. | “Revolutionize hospitality with next-gen AI.” |
| Operator nouns: messages, calls, PMS, OTA threads, handoffs, procedures, knowledge base. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/inbox) | Prefer stay/ops vocabulary over SaaS abstractions. | Draft: One inbox for OTA threads and the night phone. | “Omnichannel synergy across the stack.” |
| Agent + team split: escalate when needed; team takes over when appropriate. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents) | Name what the agent does *and* when a person steps in. | Draft: The agent handles the door-code thread; your night manager still owns the lockout. | “Fully autonomous hotel. No staff required.” |
| Channel list, then action: voice, chat, email, WhatsApp, connected OTAs. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents) | List real channels; do not invent a channel the page does not name. | Draft: Same history on email, WhatsApp, and the call. | “We cover every app your guest might use.” |
| Manifesto scene: 1am leak → plumber on WhatsApp → owner digest. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/manifesto) | One concrete night, one sequence, no extra adjectives. | Draft: Guest reports a leak at 1am; the agent pages the on-call plumber and leaves a six-line note. | “Our AI magically orchestrates your entire estate.” |
| Founder ads: short data, then the operational ask (staff for peaks; agents in the plan). [Source](https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/detail/1538381626) | If you use volume, say it is Conduit-observed and dated in the ad, not a universal law. | Draft: Message load is not the same in every market; size coverage to the property, not the average. | “Guests everywhere send 2x the messages.” |

Keep sentences short on product and ads. The manifesto may run long, historical, and first-person (“-Punn and Cole”); that register is for essays, not pricing pages. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/manifesto)

Words that show up because they are on the site: agent, inbox, portal, workflow, guest, booking, property, procedure, escalation, handoff, coordinate, purpose-built. Words that rarely carry the homepage: chatbot (used mainly as a foil in ads), disrupt, magic, fire your team.

## Tone by situation

| Situation | Tone to match public examples | Draft (label as draft) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage / product | Calm, numbered, production. Book a demo, not a manifesto. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/) | Draft: Agents answer guests and take action in the tools you already run. |
| Manifesto / category essay | Historical, argumentative, still operational. Coordination tax, not “AI hype.” [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/manifesto) | Draft: The bottleneck is no longer how fast a message arrives. It is how fast someone can act on it. |
| Feature / integration announcement | Specific verbs and objects (read reservation, open a task). LinkedIn Mews/OPERA ads list actions; an integration logo is not a license to invent others. [Source](https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/detail/1502619576) | Draft: With the PMS connected, the agent can pull the live stay and open a maintenance task—confirm which writes your stack allows. |
| Customer proof | Named operator, local number, one story. Ads retell Wynwood’s guest resolution time (14–15 minutes → 3), which is different from first-response time without turning a 4.8 Colombia rating into a caused lift. [Source](https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/detail/1513892706) | Draft: Wynwood House’s published story is faster answers across seven countries—not a promise for every portfolio. |
| Human/automation | Cascadia’s quoted mission keeps boots-on-property and guest interaction human; AI is the rest. Haven’s story is teach-the-agent, not silent layoff copy. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/cascadia-getaways) | Draft: If it needs someone on the property or in the room with the guest, it stays with your people. |

Product FAQs stay dry (“You provide policies… Agents combine that information with reservation data”). Match that dryness in implementation copy. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents.md)

## What to avoid

- **Guarantees from rhetoric.** “Build the perfect guest experience” is homepage marketing, not a SLA. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/)
- **Universal automation rates.** Manifesto 87% autonomous resolution and 95% CSAT are company-reported there; customer pages print different rates (Haven 90%, Cascadia 60%, HomeHop 70%). Do not average them. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/manifesto) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/haven-2026)
- **Staff-replacement punchlines** when the product page says escalate and take over. Easy BnB’s VA-replacement story, published on the Bali Luxury Stays page, is a named exit narrative; Bali Luxury Stays is the founder’s subsequent venture; Haven says nobody was laid off because of the AI. Keep those apart. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/bali-luxury-stays) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/haven-2026)
- **Generic chatbot / helpdesk voice** (ticket, CSAT widget, “your query is important”). Bocobay left Intercom for hospitality-shaped copy; do not sound like the tool they left. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/bocobay)
- **Invented brand-count or channel coverage.** 300+ vs 1,000+ is unresolved. Do not add channels beyond those listed on product pages.
- **Unlabeled draft lines as if they ran on conduit.ai.** Campaign rules live in [Campaign writing examples & rules](#knowledge/writing-examples).

## Editing checklist

1. Is the first sentence a guest or ops job (message, call, booking, handoff), not “AI platform”? 
2. Did you name the human handoff if you named the agent? 
3. Are numbers attached to a named customer or clearly company-reported, with the source URL in the same block? 
4. Did you avoid blending manifesto metrics with homepage brand counts? 
5. Would a GM or STR ops lead recognize the nouns (property, PMS, OTA, procedure)? 
6. Is every original line marked **draft** until approved? 
7. For ads, did you keep hospitality-not-generic without claiming undetectable AI in every market? Wynwood’s “cannot tell the difference” is that operator’s quote, not a global proof point. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/wynwood-house)
