This library uses **named case-study pages only**. The stories index also says Conduit is trusted by hundreds of organizations and by 300+ hospitality brands in 120+ countries; that is a company reach claim, not named product-adoption evidence, and logo or industry-link walls are not treated as proof. Pages were retrieved 19–20 September 2026. “Using Conduit since” dates are printed as-is. Results are attributed to Conduit’s write-ups, not independently verified. Stories usually name the platform as a whole ([AI Agents](#knowledge/product-agents), [Inbox](#knowledge/product-inbox), [Workflows](#knowledge/product-workflows)) rather than a SKU; an integration name is not evidence of a supported action.

## What the results do and do not establish

They establish, as **company-attributed** stories: high STR automation after knowledge-base and workflow work (Haven was already installed at ~40%); some operators reporting stable communication scores while automating (Haven 4.8–4.9, Cascadia locked at 4.9); a boutique hotel owner reporting lower support cost, faster replies, fewer refunds, and a cap-rate translation of NOI; multi-market tool consolidation (Wynwood, Cosmica); a named Intercom move (Bocobay); recovery from a failed AI vendor (HomeHop); and an exit/labor story that replaced eleven VAs (Easy BnB, in the Bali Luxury Stays story)—unlike Haven, which says nobody was laid off because of AI. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/haven-2026) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/bali-luxury-stays)

They do **not** establish a typical rate, a price, or that [Guest Portals](#knowledge/product-portals) or Operations (nav: coming soon) produced these outcomes. Lauderdale’s $500k is the owner’s 8% cap-rate model, not an appraisal or closed sale. The Flex header “17 tools replaced by Conduit” and “90% of guest communication automated” sit beside a 70% title, a 70% CEO quote, and 80–90% as a future build. Haven’s Guest Favorite share is 59% in the header and 55% in the TL;DR; About says 230+ properties vs ~270 in the body. Bocobay’s 80% / hundreds of hours are not derived in the article. Site widget “4.7 / 5” on G2 is not a named-customer result. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/the-flex) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/haven-2026)

## Matching proof to a campaign

Use **one named story per motion**. Keep customer, time window, and caveat in the same sentence as the number. Do not average rates across accounts.

| Prospect situation | Best-fit named proof | Do not stretch to |
|---|---|---|
| STR scale without linear guest-support hiring | Haven and HomeHop; Canbnb is separate message-volume evidence, not proof of flat headcount | Hotel groups or a guaranteed 90% |
| Absorb an acquisition without a new call center | Cascadia first PM company | “All integrations complete in a week” |
| NOI, OM, or exit multiple | Lauderdale $3.4k/mo NOI and 8% cap math; Easy BnB ~$22k/mo and 75 units | Treat cap-rate as appraised or sold value |
| Leave Intercom / unify WhatsApp + OTA + phone | Bocobay | The 80% header stat without methodology |
| Multi-country, multilingual CX | Wynwood 7 countries, 15→3 min; Cosmica Italy/Dubai | Causing Wynwood’s 4.8 Colombia rating |
| After-hours voice | HomeHop 1–7 AM; Haven snowstorm (three human issues) | 24/7 autopilot as current state |
| Stop building messaging AI in-house | Bali halted internal tool; Flex left comms out of a homegrown PMS | Conduit replacing the customer’s PMS |

## Evidence gaps

Hotel-group and independent-hotel proof beyond Lauderdale is missing from this pack. No story isolates Portals, a standalone price, or a contract start vs. result window. Index-only stories (BlueGems, 96% at 35 properties, Moxxi, Renjoy) are unusable until bodies exist. Staffing outcomes conflict in kind (Haven: no AI layoffs; Easy BnB: eleven VAs replaced). Automation denominators (messages vs support vs guest communication) are not defined consistently. Cascadia’s March vs December, HomeHop’s June 2025 vs “two years,” and Bali’s founded-2025 vs using-since-December-2024 need reconciliation before legal or investor use.
