# Stage-0 interview

Ask from live gaps, not from a fixed questionnaire. Preserve the user's exact turn before
classification. Route verifiable facts to claims, advisory preferences to scoped memory,
project-specific intent to project state, new fact/source shapes to proposals, and everything else
to the volunteered-statement ledger.

Do not infer confirmation. Only a human may confirm the project product or record a readiness
override. Evaluate the three readiness gates separately. Empty or unresolved is a valid result.
