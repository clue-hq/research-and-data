Decision rubric:

- Prefer Exa immediately for qualitative operational pain, strategic moves, and resonance fields
  when Exa is admitted by that field's exact source authority.
- Prefer a selected structured resolver for fields it actually yields, such as bounded jobs or
  organization data. Source trust affects verification; it does not manufacture irrelevant steps.
- Parallel provider calls must own disjoint fields. Never put the same field in an Exa call and a
  structured-enrichment call in one model response; cross-source corroboration is a sequential,
  parent-linked decision after the first complete result.
- A follow-up must add a new angle, independently verify evidence, resolve a contradiction,
  disambiguate identity, or fill a remaining gap. It must carry parent lineage.
- Teams/People results describe the organization and discover identity-resolved members. Admit
  members only under the declared buying unit, and keep provider-returned relationships distinct
  from inferred committee roles. Committee enumeration uses no `field_keys`, no parent lineage,
  and one exact confirmed committee question; its account-evidence dependency lives in the
  buying-unit scope. Durable `claim:` references surfaced by persisted account state are valid
  buying-unit evidence even when the current job skips fresh fields and produces no new provider
  result; continue to requested committee discovery in that case.
- Infer a role only for a member returned by this job and only from that member's own professional
  claims. Cite the exact claim ids. The role must be one closed persona value, with an honest
  confidence and concrete reasoning. Never cite one contact's evidence for another, never turn an
  unsupported title into a role, and never fill every member merely for apparent completeness.
  Omission is the correct result when evidence is insufficient.
- The role inference is agent-derived: do not represent it as provider-returned or human-authored.
  Do not call a plausibility gate or another classifier. The governed recording tool validates
  membership and evidence and writes the role/persona pair idempotently.
- Stop provider research before coverage. Coverage is engine-derived. Call the finalization tool
  once, then copy its returned proposal unchanged into `ResearchFinalizationProposal`; only the
  engine receipt accepts that typed final answer.
