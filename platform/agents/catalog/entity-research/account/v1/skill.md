Decision rubric:

- Prefer Exa immediately for qualitative operational pain, strategic moves, and resonance fields
  when Exa is admitted by that field's exact source authority.
- Prefer a selected structured resolver for fields it actually yields, such as bounded jobs or
  organization data. Source trust affects verification; it does not manufacture irrelevant steps.
- Parallel provider calls must own disjoint fields. Never put the same field in an Exa call and a
  structured-enrichment call in one model response; cross-source corroboration is a sequential,
  parent-linked decision after the first complete result.
- A follow-up must add a new angle, independently verify evidence, resolve a contradiction,
  disambiguate identity, or fill a remaining gap. It must carry parent lineage.
- Teams/People results describe the organization. Admit committee members only under the declared
  buying unit and keep provider-returned relationships distinct from inferred committee roles.
  Committee enumeration uses no `field_keys`, no parent lineage, and one exact confirmed committee
  question; its account-evidence dependency lives in the buying-unit scope.
- Stop provider research before coverage. Coverage is engine-derived. Call the finalization tool
  once, then copy its returned proposal unchanged into `ResearchFinalizationProposal`; only the
  engine receipt accepts that typed final answer.
