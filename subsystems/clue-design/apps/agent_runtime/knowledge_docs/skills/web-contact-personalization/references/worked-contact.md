# Worked contact brief — fictional teaching example

Use this reasoning pattern, never these invented facts as customer evidence.

Aster's account priority is consistent release signoff across acquired units.
Morgan, identity-resolved to Aster, leads engineering-systems assessment. An eligible
professional statement asks for clear ownership at handoffs. Keel supports versioned
checklists, named approvals and a status dashboard. An analyst's “needs a quick win”
comment is unsupported; a private hobby is ineligible. Procurement authority is unknown.

**Account page:** explain how checklist versions and named approvals can be examined
against the signoff initiative across units. Show the broader proposed review flow.

**Morgan page:** focus on a handoff assessment, not their appointment biography.

Possible headline: “Make the next signoff owner clear.”

Possible support: “Keel shows the people named at each approval alongside the
checklist version and release status. For your handoff assessment, that makes the
recorded signoff responsibility visible before the next decision.”

First explain a proposed release passing from one signoff to the next, with the
real approval view showing the named person. Do not assume notifications, automatic
enforcement or responsibility acceptance from the presence of a name alone.
Then, if this reader is evaluating fit, add useful decision material:

- Follow one proposed handoff in the authentic approval view. Identify the person
  expected to act and compare that with the responsibility the team has agreed.
- Inspect the checklist version attached to the release. Ask how a changed checklist
  should be reviewed before use; do not claim automatic propagation or policy support.
- Use a short review worksheet: intended owner, information needed for the decision,
  product evidence observed, dependency still to confirm. Leave observations empty
  until actually tested; never present a prefilled successful assessment.

The personal statement selects the demonstration and questions. It does not establish
that ownership is currently broken, that Morgan controls budget or wants a rapid win.
Do not say “we know you struggle with handoffs.”

An action such as “Explore the handoff review” can navigate to this actual page
material. A sales action requires a relevant supported offer and real destination.
Do not rename a booking form “Get your assessment” unless that is what it delivers.

If only Morgan's title were available, narrow the internal classification to
role-relevant. Preserve the useful mechanism, remove the attributed personal
criterion and report the missing depth without publishing research caveats.
