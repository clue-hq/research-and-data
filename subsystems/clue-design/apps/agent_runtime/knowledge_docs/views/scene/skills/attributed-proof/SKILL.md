# Attributed proof

Identify the exact result or quote, its source and the qualifying context. Preserve
meaning, units, time window and attribution; do not turn an observation into a
guarantee. Quote text stays verbatim unless an explicitly marked excerpt preserves
the source's meaning. A logo alone does not substantiate a customer relationship.

Bind every proof and attribution copy block to evidence IDs in the shared record.
The source pointer must refer to supplied or actually retrieved evidence, never
a fabricated URL. The schema checks joins; it cannot verify the truth of a quote.
Without usable proof, choose type-led positioning and name what was unavailable.

Make the proof the focal element with native text; give context and attribution
enough space to be read. Use authentic customer logos only when authorized by the
source context. Cards, quote marks and buttons are optional compositional choices.
Read `knowledge/scene/components.md` and `knowledge/scene/review.md`.

Review evidence fidelity and visible qualification before judging polish.
If the footnote is essential to understanding the claim, it cannot be decorative
microtype. Recompose or shorten the nonessential copy.
