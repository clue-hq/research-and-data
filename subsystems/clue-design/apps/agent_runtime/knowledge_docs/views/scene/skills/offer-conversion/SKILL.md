# Offer and conversion

Use this family when understanding the exchange helps the audience decide to act:
what they receive, why it matters, and what commitment it requires. A conversion
objective alone does not justify a discount or sales treatment. General awareness
belongs in another family; a report whose lesson is the message may fit
educational-guide. A demo can be an offer without inventing a promotion.

## Establish the actual offer

Read the current destination and supplied campaign terms. Bind price, currency,
billing interval, eligible audience/plan, trial duration, renewal and real expiry
to evidence when used. Separate a free trial from a subsequent introductory rate.
Do not translate a request for “urgency” into a countdown, limited inventory or
deadline that has no source. A savings calculation needs its comparison basis.

Choose a headline around either the useful outcome or the offer itself, according
to what the audience already understands. Keep the scope-limiting qualifier close
enough to affect the first reading. Brand voice may be assured, helpful or playful;
an enterprise consultation need not sound like a clearance sale. Use the brand's
original ads to judge directness, offer prominence and language, while treating
their old prices as historical data.

## Choose a visual argument

Consider these distinct possibilities against the inspected references:

- **Offer as the subject:** A native price, trial or service proposition dominates
  when the product is familiar and the terms are simple. Supporting text explains
  the exchange; no decorative burst is required.
- **Benefit with an invitation:** Authentic product or campaign artwork carries
  the useful experience, and the offer completes that thought. Useful when a bare
  price would not explain why someone should care.
- **A specific entry point:** A native consultation/demo invitation organizes one
  relevant customer task with an independently placed authentic product detail.
  Useful when the commitment is attention or time rather than money.

These are reasoning options, not fixed regions. Preserve the selected original
ad's hierarchy, artwork treatment, logo relationship and CTA treatment when the
request calls for continuity. Vary the supported message or subject deliberately;
do not replace the original's imagery with a flat field merely to fit more copy;
new imagery follows `/.deepagent/skills/generative-imagery/SKILL.md`.

## Compose and check the decision

Read `knowledge/scene/components.md` and `knowledge/scene/references.md` for
native composition and independent image operations. Keep headline, price,
qualifiers and CTA label native; any button is native geometry plus text.
Authentic logo/product assets remain independent image nodes. Set the artwork
CTA from the relevant original ads and the requested destination/action. Preserve
its presence or absence when appropriate; a channel button does not automatically
require a second button inside the artwork. Never promise instant access when
the destination schedules a conversation.

Use `knowledge/scene/placement.md` for square or portrait recomposition. Protect
essential terms as well as the focal object; reducing qualifiers into unreadable
microtype changes the offer. Review whether the audience can tell what happens
next and what the offer actually includes.

Without verified terms, use a confirmed non-promotional action if it satisfies
the request; otherwise name the missing decision. Do not substitute a fictional
trial. Counterexamples: “free forever” inferred from a free trial, an expired
sale revived by a source image, or a fabricated rising graph used to sell a demo.
Probe “Make an ad for our trial” with and without terms; both results must retain
truthful action semantics and recognizable brand character.
