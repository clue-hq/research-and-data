# Problem–solution

Give the viewer a moment they recognize and a credible reason the product helps.
The problem earns attention; the mechanism makes the promise believable. This
family is useful when awareness needs more substance than a positioning line.
Use workflow-contrast when the sequence itself is the message, and attributed-proof
when a measured result should lead. Avoid manufacturing anxiety for a product
whose value can be explained directly.

## Find the truthful connection

Name one audience, situation, friction and supported capability. Distinguish an
observed pain from a plausible scenario: a product page describing scattered
information supports a knowledge-finding message, not a claim that every viewer
wastes two hours daily. Write the headline around the recognizable task; support
explains what changes. Keep metrics, universal outcomes and customer claims out
without matching evidence. Questions can introduce a situation, but cannot smuggle
in an unsubstantiated accusation about the audience.

Inspect relevant company ads and campaign sources before choosing voice or art.
Observe whether the brand names the pain bluntly, uses humor, shows the obstacle,
or leads with relief. Record the visible hierarchy, logo treatment, imagery,
copy density and whether the action is in artwork or channel UI. Transfer that
reasoning to the current message; another company's exaggeration or metaphor is
not product evidence. If references are absent, state that gap and derive a
direction from the available kit and product facts without claiming fidelity.

## Choose a composition for the connection

- **The interruption:** a dominant native pain statement meets a small but clear
  product intervention, such as one authentic crop or a short mechanism line.
  Useful when the audience already recognizes the task and needs the next step.
- **The resolved moment:** an authentic use image or independently generated
  illustrative scene carries the relief. Native copy names the original friction
  and the product's role. Useful when the benefit is easier to see than diagram.
- **The bottleneck:** a few native objects converge on one understandable
  obstruction; the capability shows the change in the relationship. Useful for
  abstract coordination problems with no legible screenshot. Label illustrative
  diagrams so they do not masquerade as measured process data.

These are alternatives, not required panels or proportions. Do not default to a
red unhappy half and a green happy half. Choose the image treatment from the seller's
inspected imagery under `/.deepagent/skills/generative-imagery/SKILL.md`.

## Build and judge

Use `knowledge/scene/components.md` for measured native copy, authentic logo
placement, optional button geometry and independent product layers. Read
`knowledge/scene/references.md` for source roles and image operations. Reuse
suitable source art when permitted; requested new imagery needs a separately
generated asset, not a flattened ad or fabricated product screen.

Choose the CTA from the available next step and observed campaign behavior;
awareness can end without an artwork button. Follow `knowledge/scene/placement.md`
for square and portrait recomposition. Preserve the cause/relief reading order,
not a split-panel skeleton; remove secondary detail before shrinking the mechanism.

Check: can a viewer explain both the problem and why this product helps? A stressed
person plus “Work smarter” fails that test. If the capability link is missing,
switch to supported positioning and record the gap rather than promising relief.
