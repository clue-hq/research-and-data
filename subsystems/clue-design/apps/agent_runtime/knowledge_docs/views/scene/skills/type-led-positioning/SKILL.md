# Type-led positioning

Make the strongest supportable idea the dominant native text. Choose line breaks,
scale and surrounding space from the actual words and brand typography. Support
may clarify the mechanism or audience; it must not compete with the headline.

When the request leaves imagery optional, a typography-only result is complete
when the idea, brand recognition and hierarchy carry it. Do not fill quiet space
with a generated metaphor, fake metric, pill, card or mandatory CTA. A geometric
accent earns its place by directing the reading order. Use the authentic logo if
available and legible; never typeset a replacement wordmark.

When the request asks for new imagery, keep type as the dominant message and
generate the requested artwork as an independent layer. Follow
`knowledge/scene/references.md` for inspected reference bytes and output review.
A flat palette match does not preserve an observed textured, blurred or tonal
treatment. Reserve readable space within that treatment rather than deleting it.

Read `knowledge/scene/components.md`. In the direction record, name the message,
why type carries it, the native text layers and the intended reading order.
At feed scale verify immediate comprehension, distinct brand character and
readable support. Recompose line lengths and scale for a new aspect ratio.
