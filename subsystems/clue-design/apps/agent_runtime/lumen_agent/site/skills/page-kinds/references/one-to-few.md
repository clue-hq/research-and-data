# A page for a few named accounts

## The reader

A small group the brief names (for example three regional retail chains) that shares a problem, a
role or a situation. No set of accounts is tagged to the page, and there is no research for each
member.

## What to read

The brief first. Then the confirmed ICP and personas in `seller/` for this audience, and a few
`web_search`es for a little context. The ICP and personas are the seller's view of an audience: they
shape the angle, and never state facts about a particular company (`evidence`, "Reading the
research").

## Words and tone

- A thesis the whole group shares, in their shared vocabulary. Never project one member's facts or
  results onto all of them.
- As on a page for one account: "could" implications only, a bridge line in the plan for each benefit
  statement, a hero that describes what the seller proposes rather than a result, and only the
  relationship words `page-copy` allows ("Talking about the prospect").

## Shape

Often five to seven sections, with decision material at the level the group shares: read
`decision-material.md`.

## Identity, header and footer

- The seller's header, trimmed: rebuilt with its look and layout, its menus and links removed, the
  seller's logo and one button kept. It may carry a line for the group ("Prepared for regional retail
  chains"), usually where the menu was, or no line at all: choose what reads best for this group. No
  single account's logo, anywhere.
- The hero names the group.
- The plain footer (`building-with-puck`, "The header and footer").

## Proof and action

Named customers' results, each attributed in the same line; attribution does the job, so no extra
line is needed. The action is the next step the brief names, at its real scope. No links that take
the reader away; a customer story you cite may open in a new tab.

## The hero

Usually the seller's home page hero, adapted (`page-design`, "Which hero").

## Checking it

`page-review`, with the swap test against the group (would the page fit any other group unchanged?)
and no people named.
