# Page kinds

Each kind of page has its own reader, sources, identity, header and footer, and usual action. The
table compares them. The reference file for your kind says what to aim for on it; read it with
`read_file` (for example `/skills/page-kinds/references/one-to-one.md`).

| | One account | One person | A few named accounts | A whole audience | Event | Product campaign |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reference | `one-to-one.md` | `contact.md` (with `one-to-one.md`) | `one-to-few.md` | `general-campaign.md` | `event.md` | `product-campaign.md` |
| Leans on | the account's research | the account's research and what is known of the person | the brief, the ICP and personas | the seller's files and sections, the ICP and personas | the brief and the seller's event page | the seller's files and sections, the ad or email |
| Usual shape | five to seven sections, with decision material | as one account | five to seven sections | five to seven sections | what it is, why attend, agenda or speakers, details, register | the shortest: promise, proof, product case studies, action |
| The header names | the account, with its logo | the account, with its logo | the group, or no one | no one | no one | no one |
| The hero names | the account | the account (the person discreetly) | the group | the audience | the event | the offer |
| Header and footer | the seller's header trimmed, with the account's line and logo; plain footer | as one account | the seller's header trimmed, a line for the group or none; plain footer | the seller's header and footer, cloned as they are | the seller's header trimmed to its logo and the register button; plain footer | the seller's header trimmed to its logo and one button; plain footer |
| Links away | none | none | none | the seller's header and footer links, and a few that help | a few that help, in the page | one or two that help, in the page |
| Usual action | the next step the brief names | as one account | as one account | the demo, trial, contact or resource the brief names | register | the offer the ad or email made |
| Usual hero | the home page's | as one account | the home page's | the home page's | an event or webinar page's | the product page's |

These are what usually fits, never limits. The brief and the person's request decide.

## Decision material

Pages for one account, one person or a few named accounts are built around decision material:
something the reader can understand and examine, not a company profile or a feature list.
`references/decision-material.md` says what to show for each question the reader has. Their
references send you to it; the other kinds use a lighter version (the product doing its job), which
their own reference describes.

## What the references point to

A reference says what to aim for. The how lives in the aspect skills, and each reference names them
where they apply:
- the words, claims, headlines and buttons: `page-copy`;
- which facts may be used, and reading the research: `evidence`;
- the hero and the page's rhythm: `page-design`;
- the header and footer: `building-with-puck` ("The header and footer");
- logos, screenshots, images and motion: `imagery-and-media`;
- checking the page: `page-review`.
