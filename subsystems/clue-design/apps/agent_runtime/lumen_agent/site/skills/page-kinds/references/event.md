# An event page

## The reader

People invited to an event, or deciding whether to attend: a webinar, a conference, a dinner, a
workshop.

## Shape

What it is; why attend; the agenda or the speakers; the details (date, time and time zone, the place
or how to join online); register.

## Facts

- Dates, times, places, speakers and the agenda come only from the brief or the seller's own event
  page (open it with `fetch_page`). Never invent speakers, sponsors, deadlines or how many places are
  left.
- When nothing in your workspace gives the date or the place, ask (the system prompt, "When to ask").

## Words

- The register button sits next to the real date, time and place, worded as what happens
  ("Save my seat for 14 May").
- When the brief supplies the invite's wording, echo it, so the reader recognises what they clicked.

## Identity, header and footer

- No account markers; the hero names the event.
- The seller's header, trimmed to its logo and the register button (no menu, no line), and the plain
  footer (`building-with-puck`, "The header and footer").
- A few links that help (the full agenda, the venue) are fine in the page itself, not in the header.

## The action

Register, through the real event destination.

## The hero

An event or webinar page's hero when the seller has one; otherwise the home page's
(`page-design`, "Which hero").
