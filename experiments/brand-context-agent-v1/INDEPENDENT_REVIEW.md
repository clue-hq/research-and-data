No Critical issue was established in the inspected pilot scope. Six Important findings:

1. Compound claims cited partial passages. Pricing amounts are present on the saved official pricing page but the candidate claim quoted only a generic pricing sentence. Numeric LinkedIn ID is in a source link attribute, not the unrelated employee-count quote. **Disposition:** original candidate ledger retained as unapproved research scratch; no candidate statement promoted as a verified fact. Review package uses source-statement-only observations plus attribute evidence for identity and source-linked, corrected documents. Fully automatic claim-level entailment remains an integration gate.
2. Universal PMS ownership and action-taking gates exceeded official evidence. **Disposition:** conditional implementation readiness or preferences; high volume is not universal eligibility.
3. Booking.com/VRBO risk was written as a positive necessary criterion; direct connectors were conflated with PMS relay. Operations availability was treated as exclusion despite contradictory sources. **Disposition:** require confirmation of the specific supported workflow, preserve unknowns, separate account fit from delivery scope.
4. Haven adoption expansion and Cascadia acquisition capacity were described as purchase triggers. **Disposition:** distinguish initial purchase, later adoption and enabled outcomes; prospecting urgency remains a hypothesis unless dated evidence supports causality.
5. Brandfetch chevrons were assumed current and the SVG preview matte was described as original artwork. **Disposition:** prefer official website wordmark; provider variants pending confirmation; preserve original colors; preview metadata explicitly identifies added matte.
6. Evidence drawer omitted some citations and criterion sources. **Disposition:** compute and validate the union of body citations and structured criterion sources before building.

Minor findings: page-budget check raced outside reservation lock (corrected); workforce/no-layoff advice overgeneralized one story (corrected during related ICP revision); repeated caveats across documents (deferred to editorial consolidation after human review).

Reviewer declined to judge pending retrieval and rendered QA, full asset-classification quality, unavailable LinkedIn creative quality, independent truth of marketing metrics/certifications, actual connector behavior, willingness to buy, baseline superiority, injected crash recovery, and production readiness. Those are not implied passes. The parent evaluation records later retrieval/UI checks separately; commercial truth and production behavior remain untested.

Original drafts are preserved under each run's `revisions/` directory. No integration is approved by this review.
