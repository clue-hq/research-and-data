# Conduit research rework — 19 September 2026

This is a bounded revision of the standalone experiment, authorized by the founder's review. The platform, databases, environment and four running workspaces remain untouched. Version one remains an immutable comparison. No automatic integration or publication follows this work.

## What failed

The first pilot hard-coded nine documents, capped most at 250–600 words, and sent every writer the same broad evidence packet. Its product overview collapsed product-specific knowledge into one page. It had no folder manifest or dependency-aware refresh. Passing integrity tests did not establish sales usefulness. The saved Conduit workspace already contains separate product reports; those are a comparison baseline, not evidence to copy into the new research.

The platform's onboarding driver currently starts ICP synthesis after company/catalog research, alongside the deeper product research. That favors speed but does not guarantee the ICP uses the completed product findings. The experiment will make a preliminary profile optional and build its reviewed targeting recommendation only after product and customer evidence is available.

## Folder and document ownership

| Display folder | Contents | Consumer decision |
|---|---|---|
| Company | Company overview; commercial model and market context | What business is this, what does it sell, and what is established? |
| Product | Catalog; separate AI Agents, Inbox, Guest Portals and Workflows guides; Operator availability; developer capabilities; integrations; pricing | Which capability addresses this buyer's problem, under what conditions? |
| Brand & messaging | Positioning; voice and language; message map; writing examples | How should we explain the value and sound like this brand? |
| Audience | ICP strategy; hotel ICP; vacation-rental ICP; buying committee; qualification; buyer problems and desired results | Whom should we pursue, why, what must we verify, and what should we say? |
| Proof | Customer evidence; alternatives and differentiation | What can support a campaign claim and what cannot? |
| Design | Identity pack and visual usage | Which original logo/asset is suitable, and which variants are missing? |
| Library guide | Organization, research triggers and platform mapping | Where does information belong and when is it refreshed? |

These are display folders in the standalone viewer. Current platform knowledge folders are company/product/audience/proof; Design belongs to the brand kit. A Brand & messaging knowledge folder and asset intelligence fields would require a future contract decision, not a silent platform change. Internal keys stay stable when display names change. Product docs own capability truth; proof owns case results; pricing owns package details; audience and messaging link to these instead of inventing their own versions.

## Research and refresh sequence

1. Resolve the company/domain; inspect current company, product catalog and official identity assets. Reuse this day's public snapshots with recorded lineage; do not repay for identical ads. Company legal identity, product names and missing logo variants are visible immediately.
2. Discover public product documentation, integration routes, pricing and named customer stories. Research each product separately. Documentation can contradict sales pages; show the exact availability conflict. A missing price remains unpublished, not zero or guessed.
3. Draft company and product guides with field-level evidence. Separate actual products from enabling capabilities, partner programs and marketing use-case pages. Operator gets an availability assessment, not an assumed standalone product.
4. Synthesize messaging from actual language and customer proof. Then synthesize ICP decisions from the completed product/proof packet: company fit, professional buyer roles, operating pains, desired results, readiness, urgency and evidence-backed exclusions are separate concepts. Public evidence proposes an ICP; commercial qualification thresholds require owner confirmation.
5. Classify originals into concise reader descriptions and a separate structured Agent context panel. Ads remain in Assets with website images and logos. No generation fills missing identity slots.
6. Validate sources, document links, evidence spans, product coverage, logo provenance and real retrieval tasks. Inspect rendered output. Report remaining uncertainties and actual measured costs, not just test totals.

Refresh is dependency-driven: a product/pricing change invalidates affected product, messaging and qualification documents; a new case study updates proof and associated ICP rationale; a logo change updates only the identity pack and linked assets. Each document records dependencies and input hashes. User edits and approvals must later be versioned and preserved through the platform's existing seams.

## Quality bar

Company overview must explain identity/history, product system, customer types, business model, proof, positioning and unresolved material questions. Each core product guide must cover purpose, buyers/users, practical workflow, capabilities/limits, use cases, integrations/dependencies, pricing/packaging, alternatives/differentiation and attributed proof. A qualification brief must let a human distinguish a promising account from an unknown one and select the next question. Do not turn customer examples into mandatory employee/revenue/unit thresholds.

Buyer problems and desired results means the work a buyer needs to accomplish, why the present method fails, and what improvement they would measure. It is not a list of product features. Psychographics are buying attitudes or risk concerns supported by evidence or explicitly labeled hypotheses; professional demographics means role, seniority, remit and team, never invented personal attributes.

Every asset exposes a human title/description/use suggestion. Agent context holds observed elements, inference, linked product/audience documents, visual role, crop/composition guidance, restrictions, source identity and uncertainty. Presence of this panel does not mean human approval. The platform currently has title/description/usage_note and logo variants; richer intelligence is proposed, not already wired.

## Boundaries and accounting

New work uses a separate run and the existing $10 model/research reservation policy, with a maximum of 50 model calls and 50 new research pages. Reused public evidence/assets retain original IDs, hashes and retrieval times. Previous-run spend is disclosed separately. No new paid ad harvest is needed. Baseline database content is read locally for comparison and excluded from external model inputs. New documents remain research drafts, pending human review.
