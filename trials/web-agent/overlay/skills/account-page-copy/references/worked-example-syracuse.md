# Worked example — Syracuse City School District (seller: Garner Health)

A finished message plan and page for one account, built only from cloned sections of the
seller's homepage. "was:" shows the clone's original words, so you can see how little changed
where the original already fit.

## Message plan

- **Reader:** the district's finance lead. Second reader: the HR benefits lead, who needs to know
  staff lose nothing.
- **Insight:** the 2025–26 proposed budget: "Healthcare increase of $4.2 million based on plan
  changes and claim projections from our actuaries."
- **Priorities:** (1) the healthcare increase; (2) keep the PPO, PPO 3 and HDHP 2 plans as they
  are; (3) keep Excellus and the tools staff use.
- **Offer and action:** a free Provider Impact Analysis and a 30-minute review. Button
  "Request the analysis" → https://garnerhealth.com/provider-impact-analysis
- **Proof:** an Aon study published by Garner: employers that kept their networks and plan
  designs had 7.4% lower medical spend in year one.
  https://garnerhealth.com/blog/aon-study-employers-with-garner-lower-medical-costs
  It matches because the page promises "same plan, same network".
- **Sections:** five, each a cloned Garner section with its words edited; nothing composed.
  About 300 words.
- **Left out:**
  - Nightingale Education Group: its results came with higher deductibles and a Garner-funded
    HRA, which contradicts "same plan"; it is also 530 lives against about 4,100 district
    staff.
  - The 8% and 12% testimony figures: they combine transportation and healthcare.
  - The other budget lines: one figure is enough.
  - The deficit and a reporter's "self-insured" line: press, and it would read as using their
    budget gap against them.
  - A fee guarantee: it depends on enrolled lives, which are unknown.
  - Security credentials: the first step does not need member data.

## The page

**Header** (campaign shell): Prepared for Syracuse City School District · button "Request the
analysis".

**1. Hero** (homepage video hero)
- H1: Lower medical spend for SCSD. Same plan, same network.
  - was: Top Providers. Bottom line results. (same two-short-sentences rhythm)
- Button: Request the analysis (was: Request a demo)

**2. Where the district stands** (homepage four-column strip; icons kept)
- $4.2 million more for healthcare: Your 2025–26 budget added $4.2 million for healthcare,
  based on plan changes and claim projections from your actuaries. (was: For Individuals &
  Families)
- The plan stays as it is: PPO, PPO 3 and HDHP 2 keep their terms. Garner sits on top as an
  added benefit. (was: For Employers)
- Excellus stays too: Staff keep the Excellus network and the member tools they use today.
  (was: For Advisors)
- Start with a free look: An analysis of your specialty spend shows where it could come down.
  No cost, no commitment. (was: For Providers)
- Kept unchanged: the "more than 700 organizations partner with Garner" line and the customer
  logo row. Each column's link now goes to the analysis.

**3. How it works** (homepage three feature rows; map, assistant and photo kept)
- Know which doctors in your network do best: Garner studies over 60 billion medical records
  from 320 million patients to find the doctors who diagnose more accurately and get better
  outcomes. It recommends only doctors already in your network. Button: Request the analysis.
  (was: More data and new analytics to evaluate providers / See how it works)
- The easiest way for staff to choose a doctor: Garner Assistant shows staff the best doctors
  who are in network, available and near them. (was: The easiest way to choose your doctor;
  nearly unchanged)
- Staff get reimbursed when they choose well: When staff visit one of those doctors, Garner can
  reimburse their out-of-pocket costs, including copays, office visits, tests and surgeries.
  Your per-employee fee covers it. (was: Get reimbursed for medical costs)

**4. Proof** (homepage dark green band; photo kept)
- H2: 7.4% lower medical spend in year one. No plan changes.
- Body: Aon compared Garner-eligible employees at multiple employers with a matched control
  group, 2020 to 2024. In the first year, their medical spend was 7.4% lower, about $345 per
  member per year. The employers kept their networks and plan designs.
- Source line: Aon study, published by Garner, March 2026.
- Button: Request the analysis (was: Learn more about Garner Insights)

**5. Offer and close** (homepage paired green panels)
- Get a free Provider Impact Analysis. Button: Request the analysis (was: Create your free
  account / Get started)
- Your specialty spend, reviewed in 30 minutes. Button: Request the analysis (was: Give your
  employees the best care / Book a demo)

**Footer** (campaign shell).

Removed: the brand video, the member quotes and the resources list. They did not help this
