Buyers rarely start from a named vendor. They start from night-shift volume, a PMS inbox, a guest-journey suite, a corporate helpdesk, or a prototype on ChatGPT. This note compares those paths using only the supplied public pages. Conduit-authored competitor copy is Conduit’s argument, not a third-party bake-off. Related product detail: [AI Agents](#knowledge/product-agents), [Inbox](#knowledge/product-inbox), [Guest Portals](#knowledge/product-portals), [Workflows](#knowledge/product-workflows), [Integrations & implementation readiness](#knowledge/integrations).

## Alternatives buyers consider

**Do nothing, spreadsheets, or a VA desk.** Conduit groups a VA team or spreadsheets as manual and one-off: they scale by headcount, context leaks when a thread hops apps, and the pager never sleeps. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/build-vs-buy) Ersona describes the same operating picture on Hostaway and Guesty: hundreds of weekly conversations across Airbnb, Booking.com, WhatsApp and email, VAs answering from templates without the booking in front of them, and field teams learning about requests late. [Source](https://ersona.co/solutions/vacation-rentals)

**PMS-native or PMS-inbox overlays.** Many STR operators already live in Hostaway, Guesty, or Airbnb threads. Ersona sits on Hostaway or Guesty, replies into the same conversation (including relayed OTA threads), can offer extras on a hosted checkout, create tasks for cleaners and maintenance, and open booking enquiries on the operator’s engine. It states it does not change reservations, rates, or calendars. [Source](https://ersona.co/solutions/vacation-rentals) ProhostAI connects to Airbnb, Hostaway, Hospitable, or Guesty inboxes and offers suggestion review or Autopilot. [Source](https://www.prohost.ai/messaging) Conduit Inbox says it brings SMS, WhatsApp, email, calls, social messages, and connected OTA threads into one workspace, with stay history and booking details from connected PMS and OTA accounts. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/inbox) What Opera, Mews, Cloudbeds, or other PMS-native messaging can do *without* a third party is not in these sources.

**Hospitality point solutions / guest-journey suites.** Conduit’s product footers list Canary, Akia, Revinate, Duve, Volara, Enso Connect, Besty AI, HiJiffy, and Quicktext. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents) Only Canary has a supplied Conduit alternative page. That page calls Canary a strong, broad guest-management suite (contactless check-in, upsells, digital tipping, messaging, AI Voice, Agent Studio) and positions Conduit as agentic infrastructure that writes back across systems. It also says Canary is a mature fit when a packaged enterprise guest-management suite is the primary need. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/alternatives/canary-technologies) Do not treat that page as independent evidence of Canary’s product.

**General helpdesks.** The same footers list Zendesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, Freshdesk, Zoho Desk, and Intercom. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/inbox) No capability comparison of those products appears in the supplied passages.

**Custom build or ChatGPT/Claude + Zapier.** Conduit’s landscape calls ChatGPT or Claude + Zapier broad but shallow: it drafts a reply, forgets SOPs, and cannot book a room, push a refund, or dispatch a contractor. In-house build is framed as deep but rigid, with every workflow its own engineering project. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/build-vs-buy)

**AI-native all-in-one STR PMS.** Jurny markets jOS powered by NIA as an all-in-one AI property management system: unified inbox across Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking and other channels, channel manager, reservations, housekeeping, branded guest web app (no download required), and specialized agents. That is a platform replacement, not an overlay. [Source](https://www.jurny.com/solutions/short-term-rentals)

| Class | Buyer intent in sources | Example | Tradeoff visible in public copy |

## Decision criteria

Use these as discovery questions. Missing evidence means investigate, not reject. An integration name is not a supported action. A bundle is not a line-item price—see [Pricing & packaging](#knowledge/pricing).

| Criterion | Why it shows up in deals | Ask every vendor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Channels + history | Guests hop WhatsApp, SMS, email, voice, OTA | Which channels are live? Same conversation history? |
| Draft vs action | A good paragraph still needs a human to execute | Update booking, charge, create task—in which PMS? |
| Write-back limits | Overlay that cannot change a reservation still needs staff | Explicit read vs write list per connector |
| Escalation / review | Wrong auto-send is a brand incident | Holds, trainable review, who owns exceptions |
| Guest self-serve | FAQs and extras should not require a new app | One stay link: access, pay, upgrades, Q&A? |
| Ops after the message | Early check-in and broken heaters create work | Agent dispatch vs route-to-staff |
| Time-to-live and API drift | DIY hits WhatsApp/OTA/PMS change calendars | Who monitors connectors? |
| Proof definition | “Resolved” and “automation” are marketing words | Named properties, period, numerator/denominator |

Conduit Agents describe answering messages and calls with property information and reservation data, taking action through supported integrations (check bookings, reservation changes, payments, tasks), applying rules the operator sets, and escalating when needed. Channels listed: voice, chat, email, WhatsApp, and connected OTA messages with conversation history. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents) Workflows add a visual builder for PMS-event messages, follow-ups, and conditional offers such as early check-in, gap nights, and room upgrades. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/workflows) Portals give guests one branded stay link (no app) for stay details, identity checks, agreements, fees, upgrades, access, and an AI concierge. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/portals)

## Objections and honest responses

**“We’ll connect the PMS to ChatGPT.”** Conduit’s published answer is three walls: context (history, listings, SOPs, maintenance logs will not fit a prompt), action (a chatbot drafts; it cannot book or refund), and maintenance (APIs drift). [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/build-vs-buy) Ask whether their prototype already updates a reservation and who owns WhatsApp/OTA breakage.

**“We have engineers.”** Conduit argues DIY rebuilds when the operation changes and competes with the real roadmap. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/build-vs-buy) Unique edge workflows may still need [Developer platform & custom workflows](#knowledge/product-developers). Engineering hours for *this* account are unknown.

**“Canary already has AI Voice and Agent Studio.”** Conduit’s page concedes the suite and differentiates on multi-step resolution across systems. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/alternatives/canary-technologies) Do not recite that as fact about Canary. Demo the same early check-in, refund, and 2am maintenance path on both products.

**“PMS Autopilot / our inbox is enough.”** If most volume is FAQ replies, an overlay that drafts or auto-sends may be enough. If the job includes booking changes, payments, and ops dispatch, demand a write-back matrix. Ersona is explicit that reservations stay in the PMS. [Source](https://ersona.co/solutions/vacation-rentals)

**“We’ll move to an all-in-one PMS.”** That is Jurny’s offer. [Source](https://www.jurny.com/solutions/short-term-rentals) Switching cost, hotel vs STR fit, and channel-manager lock-in are not quantified here.

**“We already own Zendesk / Service Cloud.”** Listed as alternatives only. These sources do not show live PMS context or hospitality actions in those helpdesks.

## Proof to request

Ask Conduit and any shortlisted alternative for the same artifacts: live channel list by property; read/write matrix per PMS and OTA; a recorded multi-step resolution (not a drafted reply); definition, period, and sample size for automation or CSAT; named references in the same segment; current security attestations; whether housekeeping/ops is GA or coming soon; and whether agents, inbox, portals, and workflows are one commercial bundle.

Draft evaluation script (not a claim of fit): run identical threads—policy FAQ, paid early check-in, refund, after-hours maintenance—on the current stack, Conduit, and one overlay or suite. Record who executed the PMS change and who spoke to the guest.
