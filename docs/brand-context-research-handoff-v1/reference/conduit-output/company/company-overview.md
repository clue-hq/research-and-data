# Company overview

## Business at a glance

Conduit presents itself as an AI-agent platform for hospitality: on-brand agents that answer guest messages and calls, take action in connected systems, and coordinate work with human teams. The live product lineup on the marketing site is Agents, Inbox, Portals, and Workflows, with Operations labeled coming soon. Industries called out on the same pages are short-term rentals, independent hotels, and hotel groups. Copyright on the site is “© 2026 Conduit AI.” The site prominently uses “Book demo” and “Get Started” calls to action; these captures do not establish an unattended self-serve checkout. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/)

The company page states the same positioning in one sentence: a fleet of agents that handles the guest journey across channels, converts inquiries, drives upsells, runs internal operations, and surfaces insights, connected to systems operators already use. That is a company promise, not independently audited performance. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/company)

## Business model

Conduit sells recurring software packages with monthly credits. The public pricing page lists starting rates of $18 per unit per month for Messaging, $30 for Guest, $36 for Guest + Devices and $48 for Full Suite. Included credits rise from 40 to 100 per unit per month across those tiers. A separate Startup offer is explicitly labeled for 0–25 units at $899 per month with 2,000 credits and no implementation fee. Package eligibility, the definition of a billable unit, credit consumption and any additional usage charges must be established for a real quote; a seller should not apply a per-unit headline rate to a small portfolio without checking the Startup rules. [Pricing](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing)

These packages combine capabilities: Messaging includes Inbox, Voice AI, PMS/OTA integrations, Operator, workflows, escalation and reporting; higher packages add portals, payments, devices and operational functions. They are not separately published licenses for each product guide. The site lists operational functions in Full Suite while its navigation marks Operations as coming soon. Confirm the exact feature and release availability in a proposal, rather than claiming the entire category is either fully shipped or unavailable. See [Pricing & packaging](#knowledge/pricing) and [Operator & internal AI availability](#knowledge/product-operator). [Pricing](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing)

The company uses demo and Get Started calls to action and a separate affiliate channel. Customer stories can explain economic motivation, but a customer's support-cost saving is not Conduit's subscription revenue or list price. Public evidence does not establish realized ACV, retention, total revenue or payback for a new customer. [Commercial model & market context](#knowledge/commercial-model) separates those questions from the published rate card.

## Commercial implications

Lead with a hospitality operations problem (after-hours volume, split OTA/WhatsApp/voice inboxes, VA/call-center cost, growth without linear headcount), not a generic “AI platform” pitch. Proof that lands is named STR and boutique-hotel work: automation rates, response time, night coverage, and in a few cases NOI or exit math. Do not promise 90% automation, $500k valuation lift, or 1000+ logos; those are specific stories or conflicting claims.

Qualify the actual hospitality operating model and guest-communication workload, including the marketed hotel/rental segments and FAQ serviced apartments; resorts, villas and aparthotels are not excluded merely because the navigation groups them differently. Establish willingness to maintain property knowledge and procedures (Haven, Cascadia, HomeHop, Flex all describe documentation as the real implementation). Ask what system Conduit would sit beside (PMS, channel manager, Intercom/Zendesk, call center). Resolve availability at the specific feature level: devices and operational functions appear in the public packages, Operations is marked coming soon in navigation, and broader leasing/housing language comes from historical sources. Do not treat those as one uniform availability claim. [Pricing](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing) Pricing must be pulled from [Pricing & packaging](#knowledge/pricing) or a live quote. For messaging and voice, stay inside [Brand voice & language](#knowledge/brand-voice) and [Audience-to-message map](#knowledge/message-map); do not invent a relationship with a prospect.
