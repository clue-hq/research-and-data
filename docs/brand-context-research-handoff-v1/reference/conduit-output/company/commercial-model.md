Conduit sells a hospitality AI platform, not a standalone chat model. Public pages describe on-brand agents that handle guest journeys across channels, convert inquiries into bookings, drive upsells, and connect to systems operators already use ([Company](https://www.conduit.ai/company)). [Pricing & packaging](#knowledge/pricing) is the detailed rate card; this note covers how that packaging sits in the market and what is still unpublished.

## How value is created

The published commercial idea is simple: guest communication is the operation, and an agent that already holds reservation and property context can answer, act, and hand off instead of adding another inbox. Industry pages position the same core for short-term rentals, independent hotels, and hotel groups—after-hours coverage, omnichannel inbox, extras and upgrades, and PMS-connected work ([Independent hotels](https://www.conduit.ai/industry/independent-hotels); [Short-term rentals](https://www.conduit.ai/industry/short-term-rentals); [Hotel groups](https://www.conduit.ai/industry/hotel-groups)). Product surfaces that carry that value include [AI Agents](#knowledge/product-agents), [Inbox](#knowledge/product-inbox), [Guest Portals](#knowledge/product-portals), and [Workflows](#knowledge/product-workflows). Operations (AI-coordinated housekeeping, maintenance, inspections) is labeled coming soon on the company site, while the pricing page lists tasks, workforce and operational workflows in Full Suite; the exact release and quoted feature availability need reconciliation ([Company](https://www.conduit.ai/company)).

Company-authored proof on the manifesto and build-vs-buy pages should be treated as Conduit’s own scoreboard, not third-party audit: more than 50M conversations handled, over $3B reservation value influenced, 87% of guest messages resolved autonomously, 95% guest CSAT, median first response under a minute, and a customer base described as past 300 hospitality brands in 120+ countries and 140 languages ([Manifesto](https://www.conduit.ai/manifesto); [Build vs. Buy](https://www.conduit.ai/build-vs-buy)). Those figures are not the same as billed revenue or average contract value.

Credits sit next to the subscription. Each published package includes a monthly credit allotment per unit (or a flat 2,000 credits on Startup). The rate card gives included credits; the documentation explains consumption for voice, Operator, internal agents and paid workflow actions, while inbox AI replies use no credits. Included credits reset monthly. Dollar overage rates and contractual treatment of unused credits still need confirmation. [Credit guide](https://docs.conduit.ai/setting-up-conduit/credits) [Detailed pricing guide](#knowledge/pricing)

## How the offer is sold

The site sells from independent teams to enterprise and asks buyers to talk to an AI expert to map plans to a use case. Starting rates are shown; pricing is said to scale beyond 50 listings ([Pricing](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing)).

| Package | Starting list rate | Included credits | Buy motion on page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Messaging | $18 / unit / mo | 40 / unit / mo | Get Started |
| Guest | $30 / unit / mo | 60 / unit / mo | Get Started |
| Guest + Devices | $36 / unit / mo | 80 / unit / mo | Get Started |
| Full Suite | $48 / unit / mo | 100 / unit / mo | Talk to Sales |
| Startup (0–25 units) | $899 / mo | 2,000 / mo | Get Started; no implementation fee claimed |

Messaging is the unified-inbox and voice layer; Guest adds portals, waivers, and one-click upsell payments; Guest + Devices adds locks, thermostats, and sensors; Full Suite adds tasks, workforce, scheduling, dispatch, and inventory ([Pricing](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing)). FAQ copy says Conduit is for hotel teams, serviced-apartment operators, and short-term rental managers—not a free product ([Pricing](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing)).

Y Combinator lists Account Executive and Sales Development Representative roles in San Francisco, which is consistent with a demo-led motion alongside “Get Started” ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/conduit-ai)). Contract length, discounting, seat vs. unit definitions for hotels, and whether “unit” equals listing, room, or door are not published.

## Routes to market

Primary demand capture is the public site: Book demo / Get Started, industry landing pages, comparison pages, and a [Build vs Buy](#knowledge/alternatives) argument aimed at operators considering in-house agents. The [Affiliate Program](https://www.conduit.ai/affiliate) is a partner channel, not a SKU. Anyone can submit a referral; Conduit’s team runs outreach, demo, and go-live. Referral partners earn a flat $500, paid 30 days after the referred business becomes a customer. An invite-only Ambassador tier is described as 5% of ARR for 12 months, $10,000 max per deal, monthly while the account is active, with a $25,000 quarterly cap that the page calls negotiable ([Affiliate Program](https://www.conduit.ai/affiliate)).

Investor and accelerator presence (Y Combinator Winter 2024; company page also lists Pi Labs, Unpopular Ventures, Transpose Platform, yventures) is backing, not a GTM product ([Company](https://www.conduit.ai/company); [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/conduit-ai)).

## Market and alternatives

Conduit’s own footer frames two competitor sets: hospitality messaging/guest-experience vendors (Canary, Akia, Revinate, Duve, Volara, Enso Connect, Besty AI, HiJiffy, Quicktext) and general service desks (Zendesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, Freshdesk, Zoho Desk, Intercom) ([Company](https://www.conduit.ai/company)). Build-vs-buy copy contrasts Conduit with in-house engineering (claimed days vs. 6+ months and 3–5 engineers), VA teams or spreadsheets, and ChatGPT or Claude plus Zapier. Those are Conduit’s positioning claims; they are not independent win/loss data. See [Alternatives & differentiation](#knowledge/alternatives) for a fuller map.

Identity notes that affect how the company is researched: PitchBook lists the business as formerly HostAI, founded 2023, private, seed, San Francisco; Y Combinator lists Conduit, founded 2024, Winter 2024, team size 15 ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/548975-44); [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/conduit-ai)). An earlier HostAI launch described an all-in-one vacation-rental OS; current commercial packaging is the Conduit hospitality agent platform ([HostAI launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/KTc-hostai-the-operating-system-for-vacation-rentals)).

## Buying economics

List prices scale with units and package depth. The page provides a separate Startup package for 0–25 units at $899/month with 2,000 credits and no implementation fee. Do not present a small-portfolio quote by multiplying the lower headline per-unit rate: eligibility and Startup package rules must be confirmed. Beyond 50 listings, the page only says pricing scales—no published curve.

Do not treat those starting rates as realized ACV, net revenue, or a customer’s all-in bill. PitchBook’s public preview redacts current revenue while stating $3.6M raised to date, including a $500K accelerator (14 Dec 2023) and a $3.1M seed (10 Apr 2025) ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/548975-44)). Credit overages, telephony/SMS pass-through, implementation for non-Startup deals, and multi-year terms are unknown from these sources.

Customer-count language also diverges: Y Combinator and LinkedIn say trusted by over 1000+ global hospitality brands across 120+ countries; industry pages and the manifesto say 300+ brands ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/conduit-ai); [Independent hotels](https://www.conduit.ai/industry/independent-hotels); [Manifesto](https://www.conduit.ai/manifesto)). Use both as company statements until reconciled. Named operator quotes on pricing (Cascadia Getaways, Cosmica, Haven, Renjoy) illustrate why some teams buy—workload, automation, custom workflows—but are not financial outcomes ([Pricing](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing)). Named case metrics live in [Customer evidence](#knowledge/customer-evidence).

Headcount signals are similarly inconsistent (YC team size 15; PitchBook 30 employees; LinkedIn company size 51–200 and “Discover all 26 employees”). Treat them as snapshots from different directories, not a staffing model ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/conduit-ai); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/548975-44); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/useconduitai/)).

## Questions for the company

Ask sales or finance to map unit definition, credit burn, overage, implementation, and which package actually matches the stack (messaging-only vs. portals vs. devices vs. Full Suite). Confirm whether Operations “coming soon” is in the quote. For partner-sourced deals, confirm Referral vs. Ambassador economics before promising payouts. Reconcile 300+ vs. 1000+ brand counts if the figure will be used externally. None of the public pages publish realized revenue, logo-level ACV, or a guaranteed payback period—do not invent one for [ICP strategy](#knowledge/ideal-customers) or campaigns.
