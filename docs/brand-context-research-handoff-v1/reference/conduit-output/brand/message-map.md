## Message hierarchy

**What the product pages say they do:** [AI Agents](#knowledge/product-agents) answer guest messages and calls using property information and reservation data, take actions in connected tools (for example creating a task or updating a booking), and pass requests to the team. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents)

**How the work is split:** [Inbox](#knowledge/product-inbox) is the workspace for messages, calls, and handoffs with booking context beside the thread. [Workflows](#knowledge/product-workflows) send confirmations, check-in and checkout messages, follow-ups, and rule-based add-ons. [Guest Portals](#knowledge/product-portals) give guests one branded stay link for details, extras, payments, and in-portal questions. Operations (housekeeping, maintenance, inspections) is labeled coming soon in the product nav. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/inbox) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/workflows) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/portals) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents)

**Published audience:** short-term rentals, independent hotels and hotel groups are the main landing-page groups. Serviced apartments are also explicitly named, and resorts/villas/aparthotels may fit the guest-stay operating model. Navigation labels are not a closed qualification list. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents)

**Copy ladder:** the operator’s current situation → the named product that matches the job → what happens with *their* connected tools (only if research supports the connection) → **one** matching customer result with date and caveat → book a demo. Do not lead with G2 4.7/5 widget copy, “perfect guest experience,” or a blended automation rate.

Voice and tone belong in [Brand voice & language](#knowledge/brand-voice); the promise frame belongs in [Positioning & brand promise](#knowledge/brand-messaging).

## Offer and call to action

Public product and industry pages use **Book a demo**, not a self-serve checkout. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents)

Draft CTA (not a live campaign, not a claimed relationship): “See how agents would answer after-hours calls for this property and pass exceptions into one inbox.” Name the product that matches the job—Inbox+Agents for omnichannel takeover, Agents for voice, Portals for pre-arrival/waivers/upsells, Workflows for dated sequences. Confirm package coverage in [Pricing & packaging](#knowledge/pricing); a bundle rate is not a standalone Agents or Portals price.

Do not offer Operations as generally available. Do not invent a Portals standalone price in outbound copy.

## Proof and personalization boundaries

**Allowed:** the prospect’s industry label as Conduit names it; PMS or channels confirmed in account research *and* listed as supported; **one** matching story with printed dates. An integration logo is not evidence of a supported action—see [Integrations & implementation readiness](#knowledge/integrations).

**Not allowed:** invented partnerships or pains; averaging Haven 90%, Cascadia 60%, HomeHop 70%, Canbnb 60%, and Flex 70%/90%; presenting Lauderdale’s $500k as appraised or sold value; using Guest Favorite share or G2 stars as Conduit KPIs; implying [Guest Portals](#knowledge/product-portals) or Operations produced these results (stories name the platform, not those SKUs).

**Keep contradictions visible:** Haven About 230+ vs ~270 properties and 59% vs 55% Guest Favorite; Flex title 70% vs header 90%; HomeHop June 2025 vs “approximately two years”; Bali founded 2025 vs using Conduit since Dec 2024.
