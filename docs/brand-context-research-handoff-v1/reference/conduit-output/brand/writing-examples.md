Run one buying situation per motion. Keep headline, support, opener, and invitation inside the same product family ([AI Agents](#knowledge/product-agents), [Inbox](#knowledge/product-inbox), [Guest Portals](#knowledge/product-portals), [Workflows](#knowledge/product-workflows)). Name a customer only when the published story matches the motion, and keep the account, time window, and caveat in the same sentence as any number. Voice and promise: [Brand voice & language](#knowledge/brand-voice), [Positioning & brand promise](#knowledge/brand-messaging). Segment notes: [ICP · hotel operators](#knowledge/icp-hotels), [ICP · vacation-rental operators](#knowledge/icp-rentals).

## Hotel campaign

Situation: independent or boutique hotel evaluating guest calls and messages, not a hotel-group operations RFP. Published language: agents answer guest calls and messages using the hotel’s policies and booking details, including after hours, and pass requests when a person is needed. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/industry/independent-hotels)

**Proposed headline (draft):** After-hours guest calls and messages, answered with your hotel’s policies.

**Proposed support (draft):** Conduit agents handle voice, chat, email, WhatsApp, and connected OTA threads, use reservation data to act in connected tools, and escalate with history in Inbox. Independent-hotel pages also describe extras such as room upgrades, spa, and dining. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/industry/independent-hotels)

**Proposed email opener (draft):** Writing because you operate an independent hotel with guest contact on phone and messaging. Conduit is built for hotel teams to answer those channels with property information and booking details, then hand off when the desk should take over.

**Proposed discovery invitation (draft):** If useful, we can demo agents plus inbox against your channels and PMS. This note does not attach a trial. The public next step is to book a demo. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents)

Why it works: it restates published capabilities instead of diagnosing the prospect. The Lauderdale Boutique Hotel write-up may be cited only as a named, Conduit-attributed story (the cost path includes $4,000, an intermediate $1,200 provider, and $600/month with the described AI platform and 24/7 staffed support team; not an Agents-only result; average reply 57 minutes to 2 minutes; owner-modeled ≥$3,400 monthly NOI and more than $500,000 asset value at an 8% cap rate)—not as this hotel’s outcome. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/lauderdale-hotel)

Target evidence: hotel vs rental, whether Opera, Mews, Cloudbeds, or another PMS is actually in use, whether voice is in scope, and whether extras offers matter. Do not attribute Lauderdale’s figures to Portals; that page describes an AI support platform and 24/7 staffed support team.

## Vacation-rental campaign

Situation: short-term rental or property manager on OTAs plus direct channels. Published language: answer guest messages, send check-in instructions, and handle booking-change requests using details for each property; the team takes over when needed. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/industry/short-term-rentals)

**Proposed headline (draft):** Guest messages, check-in instructions, and booking changes—per listing.

**Proposed support (draft):** Bring Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, Expedia, email, SMS, and calls into one workspace; combine PMS reservation data with a property knowledge base. Workflows can schedule confirmations, check-in instructions, and follow-ups from PMS events and stay dates. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/industry/short-term-rentals) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/workflows)

**Proposed email opener (draft):** Reaching operators who already run listings across OTAs and direct messages. Conduit puts those threads in one inbox and lets agents use the listing the guest booked.

**Proposed discovery invitation (draft):** Happy to demo agents, inbox, and stay-dated workflows on your connected accounts. No packaged offer in this note.

Why it works: STR copy stays on channels and per-property knowledge, which the industry page claims. Named proof is optional and single-account: Haven Vacation Rentals (using Conduit since February 2024) is written as reaching 90 percent automation across roughly 270 properties, up from about 40 percent, with support need described as falling from around 25 people to two per shift—company-attributed, not a benchmark. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/haven-2026) HomeHop’s reported 40-to-108 listing growth and 70 percent automation are a separate story; its June 2025 customer-since header and “approximately two years” narrative conflict, so do not attribute the entire growth window to Conduit; do not blend rates. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/homehop)

Target evidence: public PMS/OTA names, whether overnight voice is in scope, and the motion (scale, acquisition, or inbox unification). Bocobay is usable only for leaving Intercom / unifying channels—not as a typical resolution rate. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/customers/bocobay)

## Technical evaluator follow-up

Audience: someone asking how it works, not a new demand-gen hook.

**Proposed subject (draft):** Channels, PMS context, actions, and the rules you set

**Proposed body (draft):** An AI agent answers guest messages and calls using property information and reservation data, can take actions through connected tools (for example creating a task or updating a booking), and passes requests to the team. Inbox brings SMS, WhatsApp, email, calls, social messages, and connected OTA threads into one workspace and can show booking details from connected PMS and OTA accounts beside the thread. You define procedures and which requests humans handle; gaps in replies can be reviewed, with proposed fixes tested before guests see them. How much agents resolve on their own depends on requests, knowledge base, integrations, and escalation rules. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/agents) [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/inbox)

**Proposed closer (draft):** If you share PMS, messaging channels, and whether voice or Portals (one branded stay link; the portals FAQ prices Portals as a paid add-on by contracted listing count) are in scope, we can map supported actions in a demo—not a claim that every integration logo equals write-back. [Source](https://www.conduit.ai/product/portals)

Why it works: it answers evaluator questions with FAQ language and refuses integration overclaim.

| “Hotels like yours lose X% of after-hours calls” | No | Name the after-hours capability; drop invented pain |
| “Customers automate 90%” | No | One named account, denominator, date, attributed |

| “Book a demo” | Yes | Site CTA; do not add a free trial |
