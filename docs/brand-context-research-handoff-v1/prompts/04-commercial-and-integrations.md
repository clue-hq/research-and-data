# Commercial, pricing or integration task

Perform only the document mode named by the host. Reuse verified source records across modes, but maintain distinct ownership of the outputs.

**commercial-model:** Explain how value is created, how the offer is sold, routes to market, relevant alternatives, buying economics and questions for the company. Distinguish subscription, consumption, services and partner distribution. Published price does not establish realized revenue or ACV. Do not calculate ROI without an explicit scenario and sourced assumptions; owner policy is not public fact.

**pricing:** Read the complete pricing page, FAQs and linked billing/usage documentation. Record plan name, currency, amount, cadence, commitment, unit/denominator, included entitlements, usage meters, overage, limits and availability where published. Preserve annual/monthly toggle context. Separate free trial from free tier, starting price from quote, package price from individual license, and public terms from owner-confirmed private terms. If two guides use different meters, compare execution path, product version and dates. Retain unresolved conflicts; do not add, average or choose rates arbitrarily. A “Book demo” CTA means a next action, not a zero or hidden inferred price. Finish with a quote checklist.

**integrations:** Build a useful map of systems, connection routes, data objects, actions, prerequisites, authentication/ownership where documented and limitations. A marketplace logo or directory listing is weaker evidence than a setup guide, and neither proves a customer's deployment. Explain direct versus relay paths and different write permissions. Preserve coming-soon/live-guide conflicts. Build a readiness checklist for the selected workflow; do not require all prospects to use every supported connector. Unknown stack is a research action, not a failed account.

Use the requested headings and three-to-five-column tables, splitting complex tables rather than squeezing a dozen fields into one. Cite prices, meters, capabilities and action boundaries at their point of use. Return targeted gaps and dependencies for downstream product, ICP and messaging tasks.
