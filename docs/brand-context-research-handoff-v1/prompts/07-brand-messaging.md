# Brand and messaging task

Use the mode named by the host. Read official language from the website/product pages, founder statements where attributable, customer stories and verified official advertisements if present. Ads provide language and visual evidence, not proof of performance. Source facts from the completed company/product/proof packet. Do not invent an approved brand book.

**positioning:** Explain core positioning, brand promise and supporting pillars, why the buyer's work matters, reasons to believe and positioning boundaries. Separate observed company phrasing from a proposed clearer articulation. Connect the promise to a concrete operating problem and supported capability. Do not replace category-specific language with generic AI or productivity slogans.

**voice:** Identify repeated patterns across multiple official examples: vocabulary, point of view, sentence construction, technical specificity, relationship with automation/people, confidence and tone. Show evidence → observed pattern → proposed writing rule → short new example → what to avoid. Context matters: founder essay, landing page, paid ad and support text need not sound identical. Label inferred rules and newly written examples; do not present them as published quotations or owner-approved policy.

**message-map:** Run only after selected ICPs, buyer jobs/personas, positioning and proof are available. Map buying situation → problem → capability/value → attributable proof → next step. Add role-specific emphasis without inventing the target account's pain. Separate firm fit from urgency and channel decisions. A proof gap changes the claim, not the source record.

**writing-examples:** Run only after the message map and voice guide. Produce a small useful set of proposed headlines, supporting lines, email openers and discovery invitations for evidence-backed buying situations. Include the rationale and required target-account evidence. Use only a real supported offer/CTA; no invented trial, discount, partnership or customer outcome. Label these drafts so Head of ABM can adapt and approve them later. They are examples in brand knowledge, not automatic campaign publishing.

Use each mode's requested sections. All prose rules must be brand-specific observations or explicit proposals. No generic tone adjectives without evidence of what they mean in actual sentences. Keep quotations short; the value is the sourced pattern and actionable interpretation. Do not add document-level agent_context.
