# Person dossier formation — saved-research trial

You own the durable research findings for the selected person. Convert the retained
investigation and its available original evidence into individually attributable
JSON findings. These objects will be stored programmatically as this person's
contact claims. No second model will choose, split or rewrite them. Missing detail,
compressed public statements and misplaced interpretations carry directly into the
stored result. Preserve the research's useful specificity, not just its themes.

Read the supplied person brief, identity/context references, saved person report and
claim-ready-person-dossier skill. The dossier guides coverage; original sources can
contain omitted details. Use only supplied material. Acquisition is disabled: do not
request new research, enrichment, transcription, committee discovery or publication.
Do not include a lead's contents when the linked page or recording was not acquired.

Keep source assertions and reported statements separate from your own interpretation
and research-process notes. Public statements retain speaker, context, meaningful
words, negation and qualifications. A host's question or another participant's reply
is not the target's statement. Employer facts provide context; they are not personal
responsibilities or personal achievements unless the evidence supports that link.
Do not infer buying roles, purchasing authority, personality or private interests.

Apply the shared granularity, review-status and source-reference rules. Person scope
includes public personal biography and outside-work material; do not apply the
account-only exclusion of unrelated biography. Preserve meaningful findings whether
or not they suggest a seller-relevant opening. Seller context helps interpret
relevance but cannot establish facts about this person.

Use save_dossier_section for /scratch/person-dossier.json, one independently
assessable observation or reported statement per finding. Retain coherent workflows
and quotations; separate different speakers, events, roles and personal projects.
Interpretations are optional annotations, never standalone factual claims. Open
questions, NOT FOUND categories, research completeness assessments, and proposed
outreach openers belong in /scratch/review.md rather than factual findings.

Comprehensively and critically review the actual saved JSON against all the rules,
guidance and worked examples in the skill. Review source → finding for omissions
and finding → source for unsupported additions. Correct defects in the output and
recheck before finalizing; merely listing defects in notes is insufficient. Keep
genuine source uncertainty and document unprocessed material in /scratch/coverage.md.
Use validate_dossier for representation/reference feedback and finish_dossier to
freeze the result. Neither tool certifies factual correctness or exhaustive coverage.

## Person-specific section guide

Retain useful headings from the investigation rather than imposing a section count.

| Area | Preserve and distinguish |
| --- | --- |
| Present responsibilities | Title, employer, location, dates, personal remit and first-person ownership language. Separate a hire's responsibilities from the hiring author's own role. |
| Career and transitions | Each employer/role/period, meaningful achievements and the person's explanation of decisions. Earliest indexed work is not necessarily their first job; do not apply a later title to an entire career period. |
| Education and background | Institutions, degrees, subjects, dates, activities, awards, early-career stories, languages and publicly described non-corporate background when found. Keep uncertainty about namesakes and dates. |
| Interests and public personal life | Publicly self-described hobbies, sports, pets, milestones and interests, with the original statement and context. Do not infer interests from an ambiguous image, repost or another person's words. |
| Projects and contributions | Distinct side projects, authored books/articles, newsletters, communities, volunteering, mentoring and boards. Preserve the person's own role, motivation, milestones and lessons when available. |
| Recommendations | Books, people, podcasts and tools explicitly recommended by the person. Naming a tool is not necessarily recommending it; describing a personal experiment does not prove employer-wide use. |
| Voice and working methods | Meaningful original public language from posts, interviews, podcasts, talks, books and websites. Retain constraints, frustrations, methods, priorities and stated vendor-evaluation behavior without generic compression. |
| Media evidence | Distinguish listing metadata from actual speech. Preserve available speaker labels/timestamps, but never manufacture them or treat an interviewer question as the target's opinion. |
| Conflicts and namesakes | Compare person, scope and relevant dates. Preserve incomplete evidence with tags; uncertain associations remain uncertain. Wrong-person records retain the expected identity when known. |
| Distinctive insight and relevance | Preserve the underlying source-backed finding; attach your synthesis as interpretation. An interesting discovery need not be a buying signal to be retained. |
| Gaps and outreach ideas | Unanswered questions and proposed openers go in notes with finding references. Neither absence from the retrieved material nor outreach wording becomes a factual claim. |
