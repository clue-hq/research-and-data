# Investigate from complementary angles before briefing Exa

Read the Head-of-ABM delegation and relevant pinned context. Before the first deep-research
assignment, choose several complementary ordinary `search_web` calls (`type=auto`) for this
account. The agent chooses queries and follow-ups; there is no fixed number of searches or
mandatory call per heading. Additional queries on a deep request do not replace these searches.

Adapt useful angles to the actual seller, account and open questions:

- Business and operating model: official offering, delivery model, business units and locations.
- Hiring and operating work: relevant vacancies, reporting lines, responsibilities, handoffs,
  initiatives, named systems and execution demands. Follow authors of useful hiring posts.
- Public professional voice: relevant leaders and practitioners describing their work, decisions,
  programs and priorities in posts, interviews, articles, talks or podcasts. Search by function
  until names are discovered; never seed invented people or known benchmark answers.
- Tools and execution: actual source-reported systems, agencies, partners, automation and methods.
  Distinguish requirements and integrations from use. Lack of a result is not proof of non-use.
- Financing, ownership and organizational change: funding, stated use of resources, appointments,
  departures, expansion, restructuring and substantive adverse reporting with attribution.
- Additional account-specific questions from the brief or acquired evidence. A healthcare
  assignment may call for intake and referral workflows rather than marketing operations.

Start with a modest, useful batch and inspect what comes back. Read text, highlights and useful
links, not just titles; consolidate repeated URLs without discarding fresh fields. Follow a
promising official page or job description when it sharpens a concrete question. Preserve source
URLs, dates and exact passages where useful. Separate findings from your hypotheses and gaps.
Do not exhaust all channels, repeat context reads or keep rephrasing an unproductive query.

Write a concise source-linked note in /scratch/pre-deep-review.md: what you learned, promising
leads, uncertainties and the questions worth handing to Exa. Then write a self-contained deep
assignment preserving the account dossier's eleven areas and research priorities. Include useful
discovered URLs and passages, plus concrete probes arising from them. Explain the seller briefly;
retain fit scoring, qualification thresholds and buying-role judgments yourself. Do not copy the
entire internal brief or ask Exa for a purchase verdict. Keep the nine-month recent window and
label useful older context. No downstream customer inventory is required.

The main deep call is an informed investigation, not the final action. Read its dossier AND
source package, including all source-review overview pages. A familiar URL can return new
highlights or structured fields. Recover omitted findings and pursue worthwhile questions with
targeted searches, page reads or available media routes. Another deep call is useful only for a
substantial unresolved question; do not automatically repeat the whole dossier.

Finish with detailed source-linked account research, clear attribution, useful professional
voice, contrary evidence, interpretations and remaining gaps. Do not introduce employment,
location, corroboration, completeness or presentation blockers. Existing synchronous tool
behavior is unchanged; do not assume a pending call runs in the background.
