# Copy trial — Syracuse City School District (Garner Health)

A hand-written worked example for the new 1:1 copy skill (Part 1). Written from the same research,
seller files and cloned sections the agent had on Run 3 (2026-10-06), plus one search of the
seller's own site for comparable proof. Edit freely; once it reads right it becomes the skill's
worked example.

## Message plan (what the agent writes at the top of plan.md before building)

- **Primary reader:** the district's finance lead. The insight is a budget line and the offer is
  a spend analysis. HR benefits is the second reader: the page answers "do staff lose anything?"
- **The insight, in their words:** the 2025–26 proposed budget: "Healthcare increase of
  $4.2 million based on plan changes and claim projections from our actuaries."
- **Priorities:** (1) the healthcare increase; (2) keep PPO, PPO 3 and HDHP 2 as they are;
  (3) keep Excellus and the tools staff use.
- **Offer and one action:** free Provider Impact Analysis + 30-minute review →
  "Request the analysis" → https://garnerhealth.com/provider-impact-analysis
- **Proof:** Aon study published by Garner (multiple employers, no plan design changes, 7.4% lower
  medical spend in year one). https://garnerhealth.com/blog/aon-study-employers-with-garner-lower-medical-costs
- **Sections:** 5, every one a cloned Garner section with its words edited. Nothing composed.
- **Word budget:** about 400.
- **Left out on purpose:**
  - Nightingale Education Group. Its results came with plan design changes: higher
    deductibles and a $2,000 Garner-funded HRA. That contradicts "same plan". It is also 530
    lives against about 4,100 district staff.
  - The 8% / 12% testimony figures (they combine transportation and healthcare).
  - The $15.1M medical line and the $110.3M benefits total (one number is enough).
  - The $30M deficit and the "self-insured" press line (reporter's words; reads as exploiting
    their budget gap).
  - The 300% fee guarantee (enrolled lives unknown).
  - HIPAA / SOC 2 (add one line only if the analysis needs member data; the PIA page does not
    say).
