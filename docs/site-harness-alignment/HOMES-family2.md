## C. What this adds to the skill set

- **COPY** (`page-copy`, working name): new aspect skill — claim ladder and verbs, voice and the buyer's words,
  scannability and specificity, headlines, buttons and the offer, talking about the prospect, the off-page list
  and the leaked-note test. Built from site `account-page-copy` parts 4–5, the legacy `conversion-copywriting`
  (with copy-craft and website-copy-brief), and the founder's W rulings.
- **EVIDENCE** (`evidence`, working name): new aspect skill — the five sources, reading the research files,
  facts versus interpretations, numbers with conditions, finding and fitting customer proof, grounding before
  writing how the product applies. Built from site `account-page-copy` parts 2–3, the research-file wording, and
  the evidence parts of the legacy account and campaign skills.
- **SYS** gains: the five-sources line, the research and `seller/` explanation with the tags in brief, and web
  search awareness. **TOOLS**: `web_search` and `fetch_page` descriptions rewritten.
