## 4. The frontend integration — how to make it slop-proof

The hard rule you stated maps exactly to the discipline already ratified in-house (dummy frontend is presentation truth; data with no designed slot stays invisible; re-lift, don't patch, drifted screens). Concretely:

- **Re-lift, don't patch.** The accounts screens in the monorepo's `apps/frontend/` are the drifted, slop-contaminated copies. Don't clean them — replace the accounts feature wholesale with the version from `conductor/repos/clue-frontend` (same repo seam, so this is a folder-level lift), then wire repositories. Any screen that was AI-improvised dies in that replacement automatically.
- **The backend moves to meet the frontend's contract, not vice versa.** Key finding: the platform's as-built `/frontend/v1` read layer (neutralFacts/projectSections/fieldKey-generic rendering) does **not** match the new dummy contract (`/api/v1`, display-shaped). So your co-founder's backend work is a new read surface shaped to the dummy's Zod schemas: `summaryParagraphs`/`businessParagraphs` served from stored prose claims (the Luna display pass runs at research-finalization time and its output is *written as claims*, not generated per-request), `fitCriteria[]` from fit-assessment claims (the ✗ rows are the honest-empties, rendered as negatives — a feature the PoC already produces), `insights[]` from P1/P2 claims ordered by priority + date. The v3 report's §5 is effectively the serializer spec.
- **Process guard per screen:** one contract-gap review (what the dummy expects vs what research produces vs which side moves), founder sign-off, decision recorded back into `docs/api-contracts/accounts.md`. No silent contract decisions — this is the existing house rule and it's what prevents slop recurrence. Where the dummy needs something research doesn't yet produce (e.g. the three named fit sub-scores), that's a gap-review item, not an invitation to invent. Where research produces data with no slot (openers, career history, the ten unserved champion facts), **it goes unserved** — exactly as you said, let it go in vain; the claims are durable and the slot conversation happens later.
- **The display model never emits URLs** (8% corruption measured) — `evidenceId`s on the wire, resolved by the client. Bake this into the serializer from day one.
