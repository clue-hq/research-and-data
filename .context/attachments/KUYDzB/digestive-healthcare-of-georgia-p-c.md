## How To Position The Outreach

Digestive Healthcare of Georgia runs prior authorization for every endoscopic procedure across 4 ASCs and 8 locations, a multi day, per patient manual burden for a 48-provider practice processing thousands of colonoscopies and EGDs annually. Insight Health's Referral Agent eliminates that queue (80% less manual review, $4,760/mo saved at 2,000 faxes/month), and with your brand new 4-room Atlanta endoscopy center already built for expansion, our DAC module converts that capacity into +$480K/yr in incremental screening revenue.

The flagship return on investment to feature: about eighty percent less manual referral review, roughly two minutes of handling per referral, about four thousand seven hundred sixty dollars saved per month at a volume of two thousand faxes per month, and enough time given back to schedule four or more additional patients per week. The secondary Direct Access Colonoscopy return on investment to feature: roughly twenty additional procedures per month and about four hundred eighty thousand dollars in additional annual revenue for a group that owns its endoscopy capacity.
