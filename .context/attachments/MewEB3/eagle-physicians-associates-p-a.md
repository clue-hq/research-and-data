Source: https://eaglemds.com/about/.

Eagle is itself the independent multispecialty PARENT (14 locations, 9 specialties), it is not a clinic under a larger owner, and it has no PE/health system parent to expand into. Land and expand path is INTERNAL and lateral: enter through Eagle Gastroenterology / Eagle Endoscopy & Colonoscopy Center (the flagship Fax/Referral Agent fits the GI referral intake + the owned ASC enables DAC cross sell), then expand the Referral Agent across Eagle's other inbound referral specialties (Endocrinology, Sleep Medicine, Women's Health, Sports Medicine) since they share the same eClinicalWorks instance group wide. Decision making is centralized in physician owners, so a single platform sale cascades across all 14 sites.

## How To Position The Outreach

Eagle Physicians routes GI referrals via fax to a 9-provider gastroenterology team across a 14-location, 80-physician group, every multi site, multi specialty referral packet processed manually is staff time that the Fax/Referral Agent eliminates at 2 min/referral and $4,760/mo saved at 2,000 faxes/mo. With Eagle's owned 3-OR endoscopy ASC already in place, the DAC module fills open colonoscopy slots, unlocking +$480K/yr in recoverable procedure revenue without adding capacity.

The flagship return on investment to feature: about eighty percent less manual referral review, roughly two minutes of handling per referral, about four thousand seven hundred sixty dollars saved per month at a volume of two thousand faxes per month, and enough time given back to schedule four or more additional patients per week. The secondary Direct Access Colonoscopy return on investment to feature: roughly twenty additional procedures per month and about four hundred eighty thousand dollars in additional annual revenue for a group that owns its endoscopy capacity.
