## 2_product_and_buyers
Zafran calls itself “the pioneer in AI-native Threat Exposure Management” and says Agentic Exposure Management covers “asset inventory, vulnerability detection, risk assessment, and autonomous remediation workflows.” Its launch material says the Exposure Graph maps vulnerabilities to compensating controls and that agents discover asset ownership, assess patch impact, and deploy automated fixes with human-in-the-loop guardrails. The OpenAI post describes the Zero Day Agent and says the product routes mitigation and remediation through existing ticketing tools. The explicit buyer/audience evidence is security leadership: the company says it asked “hundreds of security leaders,” and customer references include CISO titles. Named add-ons/products found in the supplied company evidence are Zafran Discover and Zafran Autonomous Workflows; RemOps is the company’s remediation-operations concept. Integrations evidenced by company material include Jira, CMDB data, cloud security groups, firewalls, endpoint protection, EDR, NGFWs, and ticketing tools. Published pricing was NOT FOUND. A definitive competitor list was NOT FOUND; the company case study explicitly discusses replacing Kenna, but that is a customer migration comparison, not a complete competitor list.

The company’s customer-review evidence says integrations determine whether a tool alerts or blocks an exploit and that customers can integrate a Breach & Attack Simulation tool.
