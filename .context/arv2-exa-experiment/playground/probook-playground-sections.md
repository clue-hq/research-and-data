## product_and_buyers
Probook says it is “built around dispatch” and that it “expanded into the rest of the front office: intake, data scrubbing, customer messaging, and outbound.” The buyer/ICP is explicit in the SDR JD: “the top home service operators in America.” The use case is plumbing, HVAC, and electrical businesses: the JD says it is “transforming how plumbing, HVAC, and electrical businesses operate.” Likely buying stakeholders evidenced by the materials are enterprise operators, owners/GMs, dispatch and inside-sales teams; do not treat any narrower persona as independently verified.

## traction_and_customers
Company-claimed traction: “Probook now serves hundreds of locations nationwide.” The founder letter identifies TR Miller as “Probook’s first customer.” Named customer evidence in Probook materials includes Summers Plumbing, Heating & Cooling, Anthony Plumbing, Del-Air, Peterman Brothers, Cardinal/Redwood-related customer material, and TR Miller; the supplied list also names TurnPoint Services, Master Trades Group, Sila Services, and Redwood Services, but I did not locate a source excerpt in this search set explicitly naming every one of those entities, so those four should be treated as unverified in this dossier. Claimed outcomes include Summers “booked 2,873 jobs in their first month on Probook with zero human intervention,” Anthony ran “10% more revenue per job on a 40% leaner team,” and Del-Air “more than doubled productivity from 10 to 22 techs per dispatcher.” These are company claims, not independently audited results.

## bottom_line
Evidence supports a New York/Manhattan vertical-AI company founded by George Eliadis, Lewis Zhang, and Ben Cervantez, focused on AI-assisted home-services dispatch and front-office workflows, with company-claimed national customer traction and CONFIRMED $40M total funding ($34M a16z-led Series A plus $6M Sequoia-led seed).
