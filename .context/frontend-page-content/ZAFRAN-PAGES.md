## What Zafran does

Zafran sells an AI-native "threat exposure management" platform to security teams. It takes the
findings from the scanners a company already owns, works out which vulnerabilities an attacker
could actually exploit, and drives the fix through tools already in place: Jira, ServiceNow and
the existing security controls [zafran.io] [AI Ark]. The company's own claims are that "99% of
critical vulnerabilities are not exploitable" and that it "de-risks 90% of critical vulnerabilities
overnight" [Press release] [zafran.io careers]. It packages a core platform plus two add-ons,
Zafran Discover and Zafran Autonomous Workflows [CTech].

Founded in 2022 by Sanaz Yashar (CEO), Ben Seri (CTO) and Snir Havdala, it is headquartered in New
York with its founders and R&D in Tel Aviv [AI Ark] [zafran.io]. It has 167 employees [AI Ark], has
raised $130.5M across four rounds, most recently a $60M Series C led by Menlo Ventures in December
2025 [AI Ark] [Press release], and lists Fortune 500 CISOs at Kraft Heinz, Chipotle, Netskope and
Ashland as customers [zafran.io].
