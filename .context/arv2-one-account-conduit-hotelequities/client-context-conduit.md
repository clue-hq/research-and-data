Origin story
Start and founders
Conduit says it began as HostAI in 2023 when co-founder Cole Rubin was managing 26 Airbnb properties and wanted to remove repetitive guest messaging. company account It joined Y Combinator’s W24 batch in 2024; YC identifies Rubin as co-founder/CEO with real-estate/technology experience and Punn Kam as co-founder/CTO, ex-Google with family-office trading/market-making experience. YC profile The earliest described product was automated short-term-rental guest messaging; Conduit has since expanded into a hospitality operations-agent platform. company announcement
Capital and early adoption
The reported April 2025 $3.1m seed was led by Pi Labs, with YC, an a16z Scout, Jawed Karim, Aaron King and other angels reported as participants. PhocusWire Conduit’s own investor page also displays YC, Pi Labs, Unpopular Ventures, Transpose Platform and Y Ventures. company page Early documented deployments include Pass the Keys (60+ franchise workspaces integrated with its PMS) and City Superhost (SuiteOp-connected proactive outreach). company technical post
Business model
Conduit monetizes a recurring, per-hospitality-unit SaaS subscription rather than seat licenses: published list prices range from $18 to $48 per unit/month, plus a $899/month 0–25-unit Startup tier. The offering also uses credits and paid add-ons, so consumption can affect effective spend. pricing page
The reported seed financing is the only funding event established here; no reliable public disclosure establishes revenue, ARR, burn, profitability, valuation, or investor return expectations. Financial trajectory therefore cannot be assessed from public evidence. The funding figure and investor identity above should not be read as a valuation. funding report
Positioning
Conduit’s own value proposition is: “Conduit is an AI agent platform built for hospitality operations.” It says chat, voice and internal agents coordinate guest communications and internal work across existing systems, with observability, security controls, multilingual operation, integrations and measured automation performance. About In practical terms, it is an inbox/agent/workflow layer that uses reservation and property context, can escalate to people, and—where the integration permits—can write actions back to operating systems. Its June 2026 OPERA integration, for example, reads current reservation/inventory data and can create or modify reservations through an MCP adapter under configured approvals. OPERA announcement V3 (June 4, 2026) separated guest-facing and internal operational agents and added Operator, which can query workspace data and generate reports or make permitted changes. V3 announcement
Market position
The closest set of alternatives is hospitality guest-messaging/guest-journey software: Akia, Asksuite, Canary Technologies, Duve, HiJiffy and Quicktext. Conduit’s own comparison says its differentiator is a single agent across phone and messaging, PMS write-back, and brand-over-property configuration; those are vendor claims, not an independent benchmark. company comparison Its broader alternatives page also positions it against Enso Connect, Besty AI, Revinate and horizontal service platforms such as Intercom and Zendesk. alternatives page
The defensibility case is integration depth plus accumulated, property-scoped context, workflow configuration and operational feedback loops—not a proprietary foundation model disclosed publicly. The meaningful risks are that suite vendors can add AI/voice, APIs/PMS partners can limit access, and autonomous write actions create reliability, privacy and authorization risk. Conduit itself advises retaining human approval/escalation for sensitive, exceptional or irreversible decisions. company comparison No reliable public source established market share or a defensible market-size estimate, so neither is asserted.

Customer segments
Conduit targets hotel groups and independent hotels as well as short-term-rental managers, resorts, aparthotels and villa operators. Primary buyer/operator personas are guest-services, front-desk, revenue and operations teams that need after-hours guest response, property coordination, upsells, or portfolio-scale workflows. About
Company-published cases report Haven Vacation Rentals reached 90% automation across roughly 270 properties while holding quality scores at 4.8–4.9; Cascadia Getaways reported 60% automation and 4.9 communication scores across 100 properties. These are customer/case-study claims rather than independently audited outcomes. Leadership
Publicly established leadership is Cole Rubin, co-founder and CEO, and Punn Kam, co-founder and CTO. YC profile Rubin’s company bio says he previously managed a $19m residential portfolio; Kam is described by Conduit as an ex-Google AI engineer and YC repeat founder. company bio
The company is hiring on-site roles in San Francisco across engineering, product, marketing and sales, including VP of Sales, which signals a functionally expanding go-to-market organization. careers page A verified employee count, leadership roster beyond the founders, organizational chart, and independent employee-culture evidence were not established.
