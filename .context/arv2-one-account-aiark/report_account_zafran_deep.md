What they actually sell Zafran’s own December 2025 description calls it “the first AI-native end-to-end Threat Exposure Management platform” whose mission is “to help security teams stop vulnerability exploitation.” Its product is now described as “Agentic Exposure Management,” covering “asset inventory, vulnerability detection, risk assessment, and autonomous remediation workflows.” Quote/source: “By launching Agentic Exposure Management, Zafran is now poised to complete the entire end-to-end lifecycle across asset inventory, vulnerability detection, risk assessment, and autonomous remediation workflows.” https://www.zafran.io/resources/zafran-triples-arr-in-60m-round-to-reinvent-exposure-management-with-agentic-ai The product claim in a Zafran buyer guide is unusually concrete: “Zafran runs your vulnerability management program on top of the scanners, endpoint agents, and cloud security tools you already own, instead of adding another scanner of its own.” It “pulls their findings together and strips out the duplicates,” tests exploitability against whether software is running, attacker reachability, business importance, active exploitation, and existing firewall/endpoint/WAF controls, then routes the result as “a single ticket” to an owner through Jira or ServiceNow. Quote/source: https://www.zafran.io/ctem-academy/best-vulnerability-management-tools-of-2026 Packaging publicly described: “the core platform plus two add-ons.” “Zafran Discover” assesses machines without installing a new agent, while “Zafran Autonomous Workflows” runs AI agents for zero-day monitoring and exploitability confirmation. Same URL/quote above.
